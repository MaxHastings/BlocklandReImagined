`VehicleView::driver_jump_held` (and `VehiclePose::driver_jump_held`, `VehicleSnapshot::driver_jump_held`): the vehicle's driver is holding the jump input or, on player-driven wheeled vehicles where the jump key is the brake, the brake. Replicated with the pose so every client can follow it (input-lit lamps with `activation: "jump_held"`). Defaults false.
