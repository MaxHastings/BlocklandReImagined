`Notice::ScreenImage(cue)`: a rule shows one player (or everyone, when the op names no player) a full-screen image — still or animated frames cycled client-side at the cue's fps, held for its seconds (`bri_content::screen_image::ScreenImageCue`). The rules tick (~12 Hz) is too slow to drive animation by re-issuing ops, so the client advances the frames itself and clears the board when it has run `seconds`. A new cue replaces any running one (one board at a time). Appended at the end of `Notice`; the matching `UiUpdate::ScreenImage` is client-only.

Addendum (same feature, still uncommitted on its branch): the cue travels as an `Option`. The client holds the board for its seconds, then fades it out over `bri_content::screen_image::CLEAR_FADE` (0.35 s), holding its last frame through the fade; a `None` cue clears the board showing early — fading from wherever it is (`screen_image(player, ())`). A cue may also name its frames compactly with `sequence: #{ prefix, count }`: frame `i` is `{prefix}{i}` for `count` of them (a still's key is `{prefix}0`), instead of listing every key in `images` — a 60-frame board then sends ~45 bytes rather than ~2 KB. With either field empty the other names the frames.
