# Design note: the fighting lane (v0.2.6 PR 3)

Scope: one visibility question (see-through paint, fog distance, target
size) and where to stand (firing spots, with the line-of-fire step-out as
its first case). Nothing else. Code references are to main at ac2bdc2,
paths under `crates/sim/src/session/` unless named.

## 1. One visibility question

**Plugs into:** `bots/sightlines.rs` (`Session::bot_sees`,
`bot_sees_player`), the one place every bot sight query already goes, and
`Simulation::sight` (`crates/sim/src/simulation.rs:1664`), the ray it
calls.

Three inputs, all engine data:

1. **Paint.** `Simulation::sight`'s brick filter (currently `brick.raycast`
   and not water) also skips a brick whose palette colour
   (`crates/world/src/model.rs:487`, RGBA) has alpha under 1. The filter
   is a closure on the brick; it gains one lookup. `clear_path`
   (`bots/combat.rs:981`) is untouched: shots still stop on glass.
2. **Fog.** The kind's `sight` reach is capped by the environment's fog
   distance (`crates/content/src/atmosphere.rs:121-126`, where fog is
   complete) at the two call sites in `bots/sight.rs:110, 152`. One
   function, `sight_reach(kind, atmosphere)`, so there is one rule.
3. **Target size.** The aim point `seen.eye - Y * 0.5` (`combat.rs:459`)
   and the chest fraction (`sightlines.rs`, `CHEST`) scale with the
   target's `scale`, which `combat.rs:176` already reads for melee reach.
   The aim error stays an angle, so a small body is missed more and a big
   one less without a new term.

**Deleted:** nothing; this adds no mechanism. **Not included:** darkness
(no server lighting). **Risk:** a bot in a glass box sees out and cannot
shoot; part 2 is what moves it.

## 2. Where to stand

**Plugs into:** the surprise chooser (`bots/surprise.rs`, `Domain` at
`:116`, gains `Spot`), the Stance mover (`bots/act.rs`, `Mover::Stance`;
proposal at `bots.rs:2991`), and the shot chooser `combat::choose`
(`combat.rs:426`), which today builds candidates from
`origin = peer.player.eye()` (`:456`).

**Options.** On the bot's planning turn in Fight, the spots are: where it
stands, and the walk-grid cells one step left, right, in and out of its
band, plus, where `Reach` (measured moves, `reach.rs`) says a hop or jet
lands, the cell a hop up or a jet up reaches. At most six. Spots are read
from the grid the route planner already keeps (`nav`), so there is no new
geometry.

**One score per spot**, then `Mind::pick` with the hold rule like every
other domain:

- *worth*: the best candidate score the shot chooser returns with that
  spot as its origin. This is the one change to `combat.rs`: `choose`
  takes the origin as a parameter instead of reading the eye, so a spot
  query is the same code with another origin (and no state write: the
  query path passes a scratch `State`). The capabilities lane merges main
  in before it touches the candidate loop for grenades.
- *cost*: the route edge cost from `route` to that cell (walk, jump or
  jet, from measured reach), in the same seconds unit the planner uses.
- *exposure*: enemies in sight of the spot, counted through the shared ray
  budget (`sightlines.rs`, ordinary share, cached while neither end moves
  half a unit), times how hurt the bot is (the health share the urgent
  gate reads, `surprise.rs` gate). Plus the ally line-of-fire cost the
  Team mover reads today (`claims::Space`, `claims.rs:96`), so a spot in
  a teammate's line costs.

A healthy bot wants the shot and does not mind being seen; a hurt one
takes a worse shot where nobody is looking. Cover and giving ground fall
out. No Retreat behaviour, no cover points, no map tags, no new dial:
`surprise.strength` bands this choice as it bands aim and route.

**Execution.** The chosen spot is the Stance mover's walk target (a one-
or two-cell move, or a hop or jet press from `Reach`), through the act
stage as now. The spot it stands on wins ties, and the hold rule keeps a
choice, so a bot does not dance between two spots.

**Second case: covering a contested body.** When a teammate holds the
body a bot's objective wants, today a hand-placed cover point is swapped
in after the choice (`contest::cover`, `contest.rs:203`; the swap at
`bots.rs:1889`). That is a place to stand too: the spots around the body
are scored by the same three terms, with worth = reaching the body when
the claim frees (route cost to it) and standing in the opponent's push
line (the heading `cover` reads today), and the chooser picks. The
soccer "ignoring ball" share is printed before and after; the swap is
deleted only in the same PR that shows the spot holds it.

**Deleted, in this PR:** the Team mover (`act::Mover::Team`, `act.rs:38`;
its proposal at `bots.rs:3053-3066`; the `place` it reads from the Fight
choice in `bots/team.rs`), whose job becomes the line-of-fire cost of a
spot; `contest::cover` and `cover_point` with the swap at `bots.rs:1889`,
whose job becomes the cover case above. The mover table in
`docs/architecture/bots.md` loses a row and the cover paragraph changes.
No "give ground when hurt" rule exists anywhere else: retreat has this
one home.

**F3.** The Why readout shows the spot domain's candidates with their
three terms, as it shows aim and route.

**Cost.** Six spots, one exposure ray each per enemy in sight, per
planning turn, on the ordinary ray budget; six extra shot-chooser calls
per turn. Checked against `bot_think_time_16`.

**Risks.** Term creep (three terms; a fourth is a design note first).
Spots with no floor under them (the grid only offers walkable cells, and
the act stage's edge check still takes away a step that would hurt).
Surprise strength 0 must give the plain best spot with no random draw, as
every domain does.

## 3. Tests (sanity only)

- Mechanism: sight through a brick with alpha under 1 and not through
  one at 1; fog caps reach; a scaled target's aim point scales.
- Mechanism: a bot behind a low wall with a clear spot one step aside
  picks that spot; with strength 0 it picks the best spot every time.
- Absolute: a bot never proposes a spot with no floor; a hurt bot's
  chosen spot is never more exposed than the one it stands on when an
  equally good shot exists there.
- Replay: one of Max's saves recorded on this branch, for reproduction.
