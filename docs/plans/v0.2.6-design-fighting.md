# Design note: the fighting lane (v0.2.6 PR 3)

Scope: one visibility question (see-through paint, fog distance, target
size) and where to stand (firing spots, with the line-of-fire step-out as
its first case). Nothing else. Code references are to main at ac2bdc2,
paths under `crates/sim/src/session/` unless named.

## 1. One visibility question

**Plugs into:** `bots/sightlines.rs` (`Session::bot_sees`,
`bot_sees_player`), the one place every bot sight query already goes, and
`Simulation::sight` (`crates/sim/src/simulation.rs:1664`), the ray it
calls.

Three inputs, all engine data:

1. **Paint.** `Simulation::sight`'s brick filter (currently `brick.raycast`
   and not water) also skips a brick whose palette colour
   (`crates/world/src/model.rs:487`, RGBA) has alpha under 1, which is
   the same test the client uses to draw a brick see-through (checked
   against the renderer before coding, so bots and players agree). Alpha
   0 counts too: a player sees through it as well. The filter is a
   closure on the brick; it gains one lookup. `clear_path`
   (`bots/combat.rs:981`) is untouched: shots still stop on glass.
2. **Fog.** The kind's `sight` reach is capped by `visible_distance`
   (`crates/content/src/atmosphere.rs:123`, where fog is complete; not
   `fog_distance`, where it starts), read from the session's live
   environment (`session/environment.rs`, which environment commands
   change during play), at the two call sites in `bots/sight.rs:110,
   152`. One function, `sight_reach(kind, environment)`, so there is one
   rule.
3. **Target size.** The aim point `seen.eye - Y * 0.5` (`combat.rs:459`)
   and the chest fraction (`sightlines.rs`, `CHEST`) scale with the
   target's `scale`, which `combat.rs:176` already reads for melee reach.
   The aim error stays an angle, so a small body is missed more and a big
   one less without a new term.

**Deleted:** nothing; this adds no mechanism. **Not included:** darkness
(no server lighting). **Risk:** a bot in a glass box sees out and cannot
shoot; part 2 is what moves it.

## 2. Where to stand

**Plugs into:** the surprise chooser (`bots/surprise.rs`, `Domain` at
`:116`, gains `Spot`), the Stance mover (`bots/act.rs`, `Mover::Stance`;
proposal at `bots.rs:2991`), and the shot chooser `combat::choose`
(`combat.rs:426`), which today builds candidates from
`origin = peer.player.eye()` (`:456`).

**Options.** On the bot's planning turn in Fight, the spots are seven
stable, relative options: *here*, *left*, *right*, *in*, *out*, *hop*,
*jet*. The chooser keeps drift, boredom and effectiveness per option id
(`Mind::drive`, `surprise.rs:448`), so the ids are these names, never a
world position. The step is the body's own width (`nav::Body`), not a
grid cell (`nav::CELL` is 0.5, too small to change a line round a wall):
the nearest walkable grid cell that far in that direction, read from the
grid the route planner already keeps. *Hop* and *jet* are the cell
straight up or up and forward that `Reach` (measured moves, `reach.rs`)
says the body lands on; absent when it does not. No new geometry.

**One score per spot, in one unit, with no new weight.** Everything is
harm per second, which the shot chooser already speaks (`tactics`
candidate scores are damage over seconds):

    score = dealt / (travel + fire) / (1 + taken)

- *dealt*: the damage the best shot from that spot does (the candidate
  the shot chooser returns with that spot as its origin, before it
  divides by time). This is the one change to `combat.rs`: `choose`
  takes the origin as a parameter instead of reading the eye, so a spot
  query is the same code with another origin and no state write (the
  query path passes a scratch `State`). The capabilities lane merges main
  in before it touches the candidate loop for grenades.
- *travel + fire*: the seconds to get there (the route edge cost from
  `route` for a walk, jump or jet, from measured reach; 0 for *here*)
  plus the seconds the shot takes (the candidate's own cadence, flight
  and switch time, which the chooser already sums).
- *taken*: the harm per second the enemies that see the spot would deal,
  times how hurt the bot is (the health share the urgent gate reads).
  Only enemies the bot already perceives as threats are counted (its
  target and the attackers in its evidence, `sight.rs:125`), so this is
  one or two rays per spot, not every enemy on the map. A ray the shared
  budget (`sightlines.rs`, ordinary share, cached while neither end moves
  half a unit) does not answer this tick keeps the last answer, or "not
  seen" when there is none: deterministic either way. Each enemy's
  harm per second is its own weapon's candidate worth against the bot,
  which the team claims already compute for lines of fire
  (`claims::Space`, `claims.rs:96`), so a teammate's line adds to
  *taken* the same way and no second cost exists.

The score is never negative, so `Mind::pick`'s clamp (`clean`) loses
nothing, and spots with no shot (dealt 0) score 0 and are not options;
*here* then still stands as the fallback the stance keeps today. No
constant is added: every number in it is a damage, a second or a health
share the brain already has.

A healthy bot wants the shot and does not mind being seen; a hurt one
takes a worse shot where nobody is looking. Cover and giving ground fall
out. No Retreat behaviour, no cover points, no map tags, no new dial:
`surprise.strength` bands this choice as it bands aim and route.

**Execution.** The chosen spot is the anchor the Stance mover walks to (a
body-width step, or a hop or jet press from `Reach`), through the act
stage as now. Around the anchor the strafe (`strafe_leg`) and the melee
footwork go on, so the bot is not a still target. `back_off`
(`bots.rs:2363, 2908, 2983`) and the strafe's `lean` (`bots.rs:871,
2969-2980`, `LEAN_TICKS`) are deleted: stepping out of the band's near
edge and pressing in are what the *out* and *in* spots are for, and two
mechanisms must not move the same feet. The spot it stands on wins
ties, and the hold rule keeps a choice, so a bot does not dance between
two spots.

**Second case: covering a contested body.** When a teammate holds the
body a bot's objective wants, today a hand-placed cover point is swapped
in after the choice (`contest::cover`, `contest.rs:203`; the swap at
`bots.rs:1889`). That is a place to stand too: the spots around the body
are scored by the same three terms, with worth = reaching the body when
the claim frees (route cost to it) and standing in the opponent's push
line (the heading `cover` reads today), and the chooser picks. The
soccer "ignoring ball" share is printed before and after. If the spot
holds it, `contest::cover` and the swap go in this PR. If it does not,
the whole cover case comes out of this PR and stays queued with the
reason; the PR never merges with the spot and the swap both in.

**Deleted, in this PR:** `back_off` and `lean` (above); the Team mover (`act::Mover::Team`, `act.rs:38`;
its proposal at `bots.rs:3053-3066`; the `place` it reads from the Fight
choice in `bots/team.rs`), whose job becomes the line-of-fire cost of a
spot; `contest::cover` and `cover_point` with the swap at `bots.rs:1889`,
whose job becomes the cover case above. The mover table in
`docs/architecture/bots.md` loses a row and the cover paragraph changes.
No "give ground when hurt" rule exists anywhere else: retreat has this
one home.

**F3.** The Why readout shows the spot domain's candidates with their
three terms, as it shows aim and route.

**Cost.** Up to seven spots, one exposure ray each per perceived threat
(one or two), per planning turn, on the ordinary ray budget; up to seven
shot-chooser calls per turn. Checked against `bot_think_time_16`.

**Risks.** Term creep (three quantities in one unit; a fourth is a design
note first).
Spots with no floor under them (the grid only offers walkable cells, and
the act stage's edge check still takes away a step that would hurt).
Surprise strength 0 must give the plain best spot with no random draw, as
every domain does.

## 3. Tests (sanity only)

- Mechanism: sight through a brick with alpha under 1 and not through
  one at 1; fog caps reach; a scaled target's aim point scales.
- Mechanism: a bot behind a low wall with a clear spot one step aside
  picks that spot; with strength 0 it picks the best spot every time.
- Absolute: a bot never proposes a spot with no floor; a hurt bot's
  chosen spot is never more exposed than the one it stands on when an
  equally good shot exists there.
- Replay: one of Max's saves recorded on this branch, for reproduction.
