# Design note: what it would do, with ledge pushes (v0.2.6 PR 7)

Scope: item 7 of `docs/plans/v0.2.6-bots.md`. An attack is worth its
predicted effect, not a flat number, and pushing someone off a drop is
the first case. Code references are to the fighting branch at 6e5e6438
(sight and spots, queued to merge), paths under `crates/sim/src/session/`
unless named. Nothing here is built.

## 1. Today

- A push-only attack is worth a flat `PUSH_WORTH` of 10
  (`bots/tactics.rs:11`, used in `dealt`). A broom shove at a cliff edge
  and one in a field score the same, and neither scores what it does.
- An attack with damage and push is worth its damage alone; the push is
  ignored.
- A shove that does not hurt is judged a miss (`surprise_settle`: a hit is
  lost health), so a push weapon loses effectiveness every time it works.
- `Capability::push` is one number, impulse plus vertical
  (`tactics.rs:501`), so the direction a shove sends a body is lost.

## 2. The mechanism: one predicted landing, one harm

For each candidate attack, the shot chooser already knows the origin
(spots pass theirs in), the target and the attack's direction. The
consequence of a push is:

1. **Where it sends them.** The velocity change is the weapon's
   horizontal impulse along the attack's flat direction plus its vertical
   impulse, over the body's mass (`combat::push_player`,
   `impulse / PLAYER_MASS`): the same arithmetic the engine applies. From
   the target's feet and that velocity, the landing and the speed it lands
   at come from the locomotion lane's shove landing (`reach.rs`, the plan's
   hand-off): the target's own tuning flown over the real floor below the
   arc, not a formula of ours. It returns where they land and the impact
   velocity (direction and speed, which the fall rule needs both of), or
   nothing when there is no landing within its limit, which is worth
   nothing. Fighting only calls it.
2. **What the landing does to them.** The harm reader from the
   capabilities lane: the falling damage `step_combat` applies to an
   impact (`session/combat.rs`, the impacts loop: `min_impact_speed` times
   scale, the admin wand, the mini-game's `can_damage(Falling)` or the
   host's `falling_damage`, the guard's share, `SPEED_DAMAGE_SCALE`),
   pulled into one rule both call. The guard's share plays a sound in the
   engine's version, so the prediction calls a read-only one. Nothing
   else: a shove into nothing is worth nothing. Falling off the map does
   not kill a player today (`KILL_Y`, `packages.rs:61`, only removes
   Add-On objects), and inventing that for bots is the new simulation the
   plan forbids; if players should die below the world, that is a game
   rule for everyone, separately. Kill bricks are out: the objective
   projection
   (`bots/objectives/project.rs`) knows variable, score and colour effects
   and no damage or kill output, so per the plan they wait rather than
   being taught to bots separately.

The harm goes into the damage slot `tactics::worth` already has
(`Context::push_harm`): an attack's expected damage is its own damage plus
the harm of where it sends the target, capped at the target's health. A
shove that kills is worth the target's health; one that lands them on a
step is worth nothing.

**Contested objectives (decided).** A shove in a game where nobody can be
hurt still has a job: knocking an opponent off a body this bot's
objective wants (`bot_enemy`'s shove clause, `bots/sight.rs`), the ball in
soccer among them, for any contested objective alike. `PUSH_WORTH` leaves
`tactics::dealt` entirely. The same value, renamed for its job
(`CONTEST_SHOVE_WORTH`: what knocking an opponent off a body its
objective wants is worth, in health), lives where `contest::engaged` is
known. It enters `push_harm` as the larger of the harm at the landing and
the contest worth (max, not sum), capped at the target's health; with no
contest it is not there. A contest shove counts as working when its
target landed within a body's width of the prediction.

## 3. Where it plugs in

- `tactics::Context` gains `push_harm: f32`, which `worth` adds to the
  damage. `Capability::push` becomes `push: (f32, f32)` (along, up), filled
  from the projectile's `impulse` and `vertical` as now. Both are the
  capabilities lane's file: asked for in the thread, smallest change.
- `combat::choose` fills `push_harm` per candidate from `origin`, the
  target and the candidate's direction, through the two hand-offs. No new
  rays: the landing reader casts its own, on the locomotion lane's terms.
- The shot judge (`surprise_settle`) counts a push as working when the
  target lost health or landed within a body's width of where it was
  predicted to; otherwise a miss. `Shot` gains the predicted landing.
  No separate outcome record. Contest shoves are judged the same way.
- Spots need nothing new: a place to fight from is scored by the shot
  chooser from that place, so the side that sends the enemy over the edge
  already scores the harm. A bot that wants to shove someone off a ledge
  steps round to the side that does it by the same rule.

## 4. Deleted

`PUSH_WORTH` and its branch in `tactics::dealt`: no push is worth a flat
number in a fight. Its value survives once, as `CONTEST_SHOVE_WORTH`
where a contest is known, until v0.2.7 replaces it (section 8).
The own-goal hand rules for pushed balls (`physical_objectives.rs`,
`hazard_clear_heading`) are a different push (a body walked into a ball)
and stay until a landing prediction for bodies covers them; not this PR.

## 5. Risks

- Fights on Close Quarters and Slopes shift toward shoves where a fall
  hurts. That is the point.
- Brooms in soccer: the broom test in `bot_soccer_match.rs`, with the
  "ignoring ball" share printed before and after, runs in the lane as one
  test if it stays under 10 minutes; otherwise the PC gate covers it.
- A wrong landing makes a bot shove for nothing. The judge sees it (the
  target did not land where predicted) and the weapon loses
  effectiveness, so the bot tries something else.
- Cost: one landing read per push candidate per planning turn, on the
  shared budget; push weapons are few. `bot_think_time_16` (under
  2000 us in release) is run in this PR once pushes use it.

## 6. Tests (sanity only)

- Mechanism: a shove toward a drop whose fall would hurt is worth that
  harm; the same shove on flat ground is worth nothing; with falling
  damage off in the game it is worth nothing at the edge too.
- Mechanism: a shove with no landing within the limit is worth nothing.
- Mechanism: a harmless shove is worth `CONTEST_SHOVE_WORTH` only while
  its target contests the bot's objective body, and a harmful one at a
  contest is worth the larger of the two, never their sum.
- Absolute: a bot never values a shove that sends a teammate off an edge
  (it is never a candidate: allies are not targets).
- Judge: a shove whose target lands where predicted counts as working.

## 7. Order

Waits for the shove landing (locomotion, `reach.rs`) and the harm reader
(capabilities), per the plan's merge order (after PRs 3, 5 and 6). Code
starts when both are on main; until then this note is the ask to each
lane:

- Locomotion: `shove_landing(tuning, feet, velocity, &ground, allowance:
  &mut u32) -> Option<(Vec3 landing, Vec3 impact)>`; `None` is no landing
  within the limit (worth 0). The allowance (motor ticks) is a share of the
  bots' combat `Budget`, and what it used is charged back, so there is
  still one budget. The impact is the hardest hit of the whole flight.
- Capabilities: `fall_harm(&Session, owner, impact: Vec3) -> f32`,
  read-only, the rule `step_combat` applies too; `Capability::push`
  becomes `(along, up)`; `Context::push_harm: f32`.

## 8. For v0.2.7

- Replace the contest worth with the landing's distance from the
  contested body, and delete `CONTEST_SHOVE_WORTH`.
