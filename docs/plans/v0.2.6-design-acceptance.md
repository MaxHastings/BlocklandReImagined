# v0.2.6 acceptance run: design (capabilities half)

Plan row 9 and §7 of `docs/plans/v0.2.6-bots.md`. The review thread writes
the spec and judges the results; locomotion and fighting hand over the
body and ledge halves; this lane writes the package and the test. Written
2026-10-07 on top of timed throws (`c424c8b0`). No code yet.

## What it proves

That content no production code knows, expressed in the platform's own
vocabulary, needs no bot-specific code: bots fight with an unfamiliar
fused weapon and an unfamiliar push weapon, and move an unfamiliar loose
body to where the rules want it, under several seeds, with every check an
absolute (something happens at least once, or never happens).

## 1. The package, built in the test

One synthetic package, built in code at test time (as
`bot_creator_heldout.rs` builds its held-out package), never committed as
data any engine file could name:

- **Names no code knows.** Every id is made from a namespace and words
  chosen per variant (two variants, as `held_out_..._in_two_worlds` does),
  so a name check anywhere would pass one variant and fail the other.
- **Fused weapon** (mine): an image that cooks on the press and throws on
  the same press (`cook.script` is its fire state's script; not held, so
  not cooking), a ballistic bouncing projectile that is never armed by a
  hit, a blast, harmful shards on the burst and a harmless trail in
  flight. Fuse length, bounce and blast are drawn per seed from ranges
  that keep it a grenade.
- **Push weapon** (mine): a short-range projectile with impulse and lift
  and no damage. Its worth comes from where the push lands (fighting's
  consequence worth, row 7); before row 7 lands it is worth
  `PUSH_WORTH`, and the checks below hold either way.
- **Loose objective body** (mine): a ball-family body the package's rules
  want inside a zone (a desired state, as the soccer kit declares), with
  no item, score or goal named anywhere bots read.
- **Handed over by the other lanes:** the odd body (gravity, jump, jet
  numbers; locomotion), raised platforms and a drop that hurts (the ledge
  half; fighting), a see-through barrier (fighting), an odd vehicle
  (locomotion). Each arrives as a builder function in the same module, so
  the test composes them without knowing what they hold.

Code: the builder in `crates/chaos/src/unfamiliar.rs` (one function per
part), the test in `crates/chaos/tests/acceptance_unfamiliar.rs`.

## 2. The run

- Two teams of bots (no humans) on a flat slate with the handed-over
  geometry, each bot spawned with both weapons.
- Seeds: three per variant, so six runs. A seed picks the weapon numbers
  above, the spawn spots and the bots' own seeded drift.
- Length: 60 seconds of game time a run, well inside the 10-minute rule
  for all six together in release.
- Every run is recorded with `bri_sim::replay::Recorder` and played back
  in the same test; the run passes only if the replay matches tick for
  tick (same build, same machine, as replay promises).

## 3. The checks (absolutes only)

Mine:
- The fused weapon is thrown at least once in each run, and at least once
  across a variant it goes off within its blast of an enemy.
- It never goes off within its danger zone (blast plus harmful shards'
  reach) of the thrower or an ally.
- The push weapon is fired at least once and moves an opponent at least
  once.
- The objective body enters its zone at least once per variant.

Handed over (the review thread owns their wording): a bot gets up the
platforms; sees someone through the barrier; pushes someone off the drop
at least once and never a teammate; nothing is stuck for the whole run.

Printed, not gated: weapon shares, throws per bot, pushes, time to the
first objective entry.

## 4. What it does not do

No tuning numbers are asserted, no rates or shares are gated, and no
fixture injects targets, routes or controls. If a check fails, the answer
is one of the contract's three (the content must declare it, a shared
mechanism has a bug, or a new general affordance), never a bot behaviour
that knows the package.

## 5. Questions for review

- Two variants by three seeds, or more seeds and one variant?
- Does the review thread want the objective body's zone entry as an
  absolute here, or only "moves the objective" (it moved at all)?
