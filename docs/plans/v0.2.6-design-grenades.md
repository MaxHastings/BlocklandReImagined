# v0.2.6 timed throws (grenades): design

Plan row 5, item 6 of `docs/plans/v0.2.6-bots.md`. This builds on §2 of
`docs/plans/v0.2.6-design-capabilities.md`, which the review approved with
two decisions: children count as zero damage but widen the danger zone; the
fuse budget is derived from the projectile's own data, with no new
constant. Written 2026-10-07 against loop 3 (`498d7f58`) and the fighting
branch (`b6280a24`: `tactics::worth`/`rate`, `combat::Choice::{dealt,
seconds}`, `Budget::has_turn`). No code yet.

## What a player sees

A bot holding an Add-On grenade throws it so it goes off near you: lobbed
over a wall, bounced round a corner, or rolled to your feet. It never throws
one that would go off on itself or a teammate. Stock v20 weapons play as
they do today unless the vanilla check below says otherwise.

## 1. The delivery

`tactics::Delivery` gains one kind:

```rust
/// Flies, bounces and goes off on its own clock (a grenade).
Timed { flight: Flight, fuse_ticks: u32 },
```

`native_capability` picks it from data alone, no names:

- the image has `cook` (the fuse rides the throw), or
- the projectile is `ballistic`, not `explode_player`, and `arm_ticks > 0`
  (it bounces before it can burst).

`fuse_ticks` is when it goes off, read in the runtime's own order:

- a cooked image: `Cook::fuse_ticks` minus the ticks its states spend from
  the pin-drop state (`Cook::script`) to the fire state, walked the way
  `cadence()` already walks the states;
- otherwise `lifetime_ticks` when `explode_death`;
- `max_bounces` ends it early, and `arm_ticks` decides whether a hit
  before then bursts or bounces (`bursts`, below).

A projectile that would only fade at the end of its life and never bursts
stays refused.

## 2. One physics model

Two functions come out of `WeaponsWorld::projectile_step`
(`weapons/src/runtime.rs`, the bounce at the `elasticity`/`friction`
reflection and the burst checks around it) into public functions in
`bri_weapons::runtime`, and the runtime calls them in place of its own
copies:

- `bounce(def, velocity, normal) -> Vec3`: the reflected velocity, and
  whether it sticks (`min_stick_speed`, `bounce_angle`);
- `bursts(def, age, bounces, hit, fuse) -> bool`: goes off now (fuse out,
  end of life with `explode_death`, armed hit, a player hit with
  `explode_player`, `max_bounces` reached).

A unit test in `bri-weapons` steps one projectile through the runtime and
through the two functions and checks they agree tick for tick.

## 3. Where it goes off

`combat.rs` already checks a curved shot's path tick by tick against
`WeaponQuery::passage` (`clear_path`). For `Timed` the same walk continues
past a hit:

- between hits, positions come from `tactics::flight_position`, exact per
  tick;
- at a hit it applies `bounce`, restarts the arc from the hit with the new
  velocity, counts the bounce and asks `bursts`;
- it stops where `bursts` says, giving the burst point and tick.

**Ray budget, from the projectile's own fall.** A long fuse
(`fuse_ticks` can exceed `PATH_TICKS`) is checked in chords of several
ticks. The chord length is the largest number of ticks `k` whose arc sags
less than the world's thinnest brick (a plate) off the straight chord:
`fall_per_tick * k^2 / 8 < plate`. A fast flat throw gets long chords, a
lofted one short ones. Projectiles sweep as rays in the runtime (they have
no size of their own), so the brick grid's plate height, which the world
already defines, is the tolerance rather than a projectile radius. **For
review:** the approved condition said "the projectile's own gravity and
size"; this is the closest honest reading, and I'll use whichever the
reviewer prefers. Rays come from the shared budget; a walk that runs out
continues on the next planning turn, as curved shots already do
(`pending`).

## 4. Aim and worth

- **Candidates:** the aims the splash chooser already makes (the body, the
  feet, a surface beside the target, `variant`), each on the low and the
  high arc that `InterceptSearch` already returns. No new aim search.
- **The check:** the throw counts when the burst point is within the splash
  radius of where the target will be at the burst tick (its velocity
  carried forward, as `variant` does for impacts). This replaces the
  first-impact distance check for `Timed` only.
- **Worth:** `tactics::worth` with the burst point as the impact and the
  burst time as the flight time, so a long fuse costs seconds and scores
  lower than a direct shot that does the same.
- **Safety:** `safe_blast` and `clearances` take the burst point and tick.
  The danger zone is the splash radius widened by how far the children can
  fly, read from the children's own data (`speed * lifetime_ticks / HZ` of
  each child projectile, plus that child's own explosion radius); a child
  set with no lifetime is still refused. Children count as zero damage, so
  flak and cluster weapons also become usable, valued by their main burst.
  Auras (fire, gas) stay refused.
- **Firing:** unchanged executor. A bot throws the moment the states allow;
  it never holds the pin. Cooking is out of this version.

## 5. What it deletes

- The `cook` refusal and the `children` refusal in `native_capability`
  (`tactics.rs`, `DescriptorRequired::SecondaryEffects` for those two
  cases).
- The runtime's inline copies of the bounce and burst rules (they become
  the two shared functions).
- The first-impact splash check in `variant` for timed deliveries.

## 6. Proof

- `tactics` unit tests: `Delivery::Timed` validation, the fuse read for a
  cooked and an uncooked grenade, the chord length from fall.
- `bri-weapons`: runtime and shared functions agree tick for tick (§2).
- A chaos test on a synthetic package with no stock or Add-On names: a
  fused, bouncing, child-throwing weapon; bots in a room with a corner and
  a low wall; several seeds. Absolutes only: a bot throws it at least once;
  at least once it goes off within its splash radius of an enemy; it never
  goes off within the danger zone of the thrower or an ally.
- The vanilla check: before merge I list every vanilla projectile the new
  rule picks up and say in the PR whether bots now use any stock weapon
  differently. Then one Add-On grenade from Max's set (the explosive pack
  port, which uses `max_bounces`) is checked headless on regenerated
  content.
- `bot_think_time_16` stays under its bar.
