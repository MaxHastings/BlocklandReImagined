# Design note: expected harm to the bot's own side, for every shot

Scope: one mechanism that answers "what would this shot do to my side",
for hand weapons, pellets, arcs, explosions, vehicle guns and timed
throws alike, from weapon data only. Read from
`claude/v0.2.6-fighting-pushes-ki8k6d` at 107dd55 (which carries
`fire::clear_cone` and capabilities d233e7fc). Paths are under
`crates/sim/src/session/bots/`. No code.

## 1. What exists today, case by case

There are two checks, and they do not agree with each other.

**At planning** (`combat::choose`, once per planning turn, per candidate
weapon and aim):
- `clear_path` (`combat.rs`) steps the shot's real flight chord by chord
  (a ray per chord) and refuses the candidate if any ally body, grown by
  its motion over the flight, lies on a chord.
- `safe_blast` plus `suitability` (`tactics.rs:725`) refuse a splash
  weapon outright (`Unsuited::UnsafeBlast`) when the impact lies within
  `splash_radius + danger + 1` of the bot or any ally: a yes/no at a
  fixed distance, with no falloff and no weighing against the enemy harm.

**At the trigger** (`bot_shot`, every tick): `bot_fire_clear`
(`interactions.rs:1260`) holds fire if any ally body is inside a straight
cone (`shot_space`) from the eye to the aim point and on to the weapon's
reach, of radius `splash` the whole way, widening by the aim error plus
`clear_cone(spread, pellets)`. Yes/no again. The same cone is published as
`Shot.harm` (`claims::Space`) so teammates step out of it.

| Case | What happens | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Hitscan, one projectile (gun, bow) | chords + cone | the cone widens by `0.6 × spread` (`SPREAD_CLEAR`): a patch |
| Pellets (shotgun) | cone widened to the full spread when `projectiles > 1` | still yes/no: one pellet that might graze holds all of them; nothing says how many would hit |
| Arcs (rocket, bow, spear) | chords follow the arc at planning; the trigger cone is a straight line | a rocket arcing over a teammate is held at the trigger though its flight clears; one landing beside a teammate past the aim point is not |
| Splash | distance test at planning; `splash` radius along the whole line at the trigger | no falloff (the host's own rule is quadratic, `weapons/runtime.rs:4589`); a rocket flying past a teammate mid-flight is held though it bursts far away |
| Vehicle guns (tank shell, jeep gun) | `choose` returns `Unsupported` for a mounted bot, so only the trigger cone runs, with a `Weapon` from the gun's projectile (`fire.rs:203`: spread 0, splash = explosion radius) | no arc, no falloff, no chords; the same for a bot gunner on a player's vehicle or its own |
| Timed throws (grenades) | `burst()` (`combat.rs:1043`) follows bounces and the fuse to the burst point; `safe_blast` tests there | `clear_path` is skipped for timed deliveries (review-timed-throws.md), so an ally standing in the throw's flight is not seen; the trigger cone is a straight line to a lobbed aim point, meaningless for a lob |
| Melee (hammer, sword) | `native_hammer_target` must return the enemy | fine |
| Push weapons (broom) | nothing for allies | an ally in the swing is never considered |
| Friendly fire off | both checks still hold fire | the rules say the shot cannot hurt a teammate, so a player would take it; bots do not |

## 2. One mechanism: expected harm

One function, `shot_harm(choice, bodies, rules) -> Harm { enemy, ally,
own }`, in health points, run where `clear_path` and `safe_blast` run
today, with the same chord rays. It does three things in order.

**Where it goes.** The path the shot already predicts:
- Ray: the line to its reach. Projectile: `flight_position` chord by
  chord. Timed: `burst()`, bounces and fuse included. Contact: the hammer
  target. A vehicle gun gets a `Capability` from its projectile data (the
  fields `bot_vehicle_weapon` already reads: projectile, speed, charge,
  fall), so a mounted bot goes through `choose` like a hand weapon and the
  `Unsupported` return for mounts goes. Bots in players' vehicles and in
  their own take the same path; the only difference is who sits beside
  them, and crew of the same vehicle are spared as today.
- The first body on a chord ends the path there (a Ray or Projectile
  stops in it; a Timed throw asks `rebound`, as the host would). Bodies
  are the bot, its allies and its enemies, each grown by its motion over
  the flight, as `clear_path` grows allies now.

**What it does there.** From the data the capability already carries:
- *Direct hit* on the body the path ends in: `direct_damage` times the
  share of projectiles that hit. For one projectile that share is 1. For
  pellets it is the body's angular size over the spread cone's at that
  distance, times the count, capped at 1: a teammate filling a third of
  the cone takes a third of the pellets. No `SPREAD_CLEAR`.
- *Splash* at the end point, to every body within `splash_radius`:
  `splash_damage × (1 − (d/r)²)`, the host's own falloff
  (`weapons/runtime.rs:4589`), exported from `bri_weapons::runtime` as one
  function so bots and the host cannot disagree. A body within `danger`
  (child fragments, whose damage the capability cannot predict) counts as
  the full `splash_damage`, which is the rule the `danger` field encodes
  today.
- *Own harm* honours the projectile's self-damage flag the host honours
  (`runtime.rs:292`), so a weapon that cannot hurt its owner scores no own
  harm.
- *Rules first.* Each body's harm is multiplied by whether the rules let
  this shot hurt it (`minigames.can_damage`, the same question the host
  asks when it lands). With friendly fire off, ally harm is 0 and the bot
  shoots past its teammates as a player does. Spawn protection and
  mini-game membership come for free the same way.

**What it is worth.** `suitability` already returns `(damage, seconds)`.
Damage becomes `enemy − ally − own`, all health points, so one point of a
teammate's health is worth one point of an enemy's: no weight. Two
absolutes with no number in them: a shot whose expected ally or own harm
is at least what that body has left (it would kill) is refused outright,
and a shot with nothing left after the subtraction scores nothing and is
no option. The chooser then trades as it does for everything else: a
rocket at an enemy standing beside a teammate loses to the gun, and when
only the rocket is in hand it waits for the teammate to clear, which is
what the hold rule's paused-weapon state already does.

**At the trigger.** The same `Harm` is computed on the planning turn and
kept with the choice; between turns the trigger re-tests only the cheap
part each tick, without rays: whether any body has moved into the
predicted path tube or burst sphere (`Space::holds`, which exists). So the
per-tick cost is today's. `Shot.harm` (the `Space` teammates step out of)
is built from the predicted path and the burst sphere at its end instead
of a splash-wide cone along the whole line, so teammates stop stepping
out of a rocket's flight and step out of where it bursts.

## 3. What it deletes

- `fire::clear_cone` and `SPREAD_CLEAR` (`bots.rs:792`).
- `shot_space`'s cone and `bot_fire_clear` as a separate judge
  (`interactions.rs:112, 1260`): the trigger re-tests the planned path.
- `safe_blast`, `clearances`, `Context::{self_clearance, ally_clearance,
  blast_margin}` and `Unsuited::UnsafeBlast` (`combat.rs:1420-1441`,
  `tactics.rs:725`): one `Harm` in their place.
- The ally envelope loop inside `clear_path`: the body walk is the path
  walk.
- `choose`'s `Unsupported` for mounted bots (`combat.rs:443`) and the
  mount-only trigger pulse that stood in for it: vehicle guns are
  capabilities.
- The `clear_path` skip for timed deliveries: a throw's flight is walked
  like any other.

Nothing names a weapon. The inputs are `direct_damage`, `splash_damage`,
`splash_radius`, `danger`, `spread`, `projectiles`, the flight, the fuse
and bounces, the self-damage flag and the rules' answer.

## 4. Cost

`bot_think_time_16` sits at 595 µs a tick against the 2000 µs bar. Rays
are the dear part, and this casts the same chords `clear_path` casts
today; the body walk and the falloff pass are arithmetic over at most 32
bodies per candidate. What is new:
- vehicle gunners now plan through `choose` (chord rays they did not cast;
  bounded by `PATH_TICKS` per candidate, one candidate per gun);
- timed throws walk their flight (the chord count the timed-throws review
  already measured at 507 µs with a grenade in play).
Expected: tens of µs more per tick with 16 bots. The PR re-measures and
the bar holds.

## 5. Sanity tests

- Mechanism: a teammate filling half the spread cone takes half the
  pellets' damage, one of them none; a splash at the host's falloff gives
  the same number the host deals.
- Mechanism: with friendly fire off, a bot whose teammate stands in its
  line fires; with it on, the same shot is no option.
- Absolute: no planned shot is ever expected to kill an ally (over seeds).
- Absolute: a tank gunner never fires a shell whose burst would hurt its
  own crew or an ally more than the enemy.
- Absolute: an ally standing where a timed throw would land makes that
  throw no option; one standing under its arc does not.

## 6. In v0.2.6, and the one piece that waits

Max chose v0.2.6 (16:22Z). Everything above is one change by one lane and
goes in, the pellet patch going out with it.

One piece waits for v0.2.7, with its reason: **push harm to allies** (an
explosion's impulse, or a broom swing, sending a teammate off a ledge).
The prediction exists for the target (ledge pushes), so it is a function
call per ally body in the impulse radius, but it multiplies the landing
predictions per candidate and changes blast-near-ledge fights in a way
nobody has watched yet. It is one line to add once the rest is in and
measured; the note records it so nobody writes a second rule for it.
