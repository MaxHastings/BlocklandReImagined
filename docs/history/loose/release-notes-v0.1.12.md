## BlocklandReImagined v0.1.12

### New
- Blockhead Bot is smarter. It chases you while jetting through the air, grabs, carries and throws things with the Gravity Gun, and decides what to do with a new, cleaner brain.
- Portals feel like part of the world. Aiming, ghost bricks, building and tools all work through portals, name tags follow players through them, and far-away portals stay live.
- New fog, sky and horizon. Distant bricks, players, vehicles and particles fade smoothly into the sky.
- Smooth day and night. The sun and shadows now move smoothly instead of ticking.
- Simpler lighting menu. Unified and Unified + Shine are now one Unified option.
- Dedicated server: the Windows and Linux downloads now include `bri-server`. Start it with a map name; admin passwords and settings live in its `server.json`, and admins can change the map from the Admin menu. See `DEDICATED-SERVER.md`.
- More bundled Tier+Tactical Add-Ons, turned off to start: Kai's Short Rifle, the AddAmmo event, and Frog's Weaponry and its WWII pack.
- Slayer and Capture the Flag: brick prints, Team_ bricks, region boundaries, team vehicles, capture points that repaint, revive on reset, and every flag type, picked with the host's Flag Type setting.

### Fixes
- The game no longer asks you to update when you already have the latest version.
- The Duplicator's firing sparkle shows again.
- The Destructo Wand and Add-On Settings buttons in the Admin menu no longer overlap.
- Clicking with an empty hand and then taking out a tool fires the tool.
- Items held part way through a portal draw on both sides.
- Imported v20 saves show their pictures.

### Known issues
- Flipping a car doesn't aim through portals yet.
- Dedicated servers don't autosave yet, and there is no Mac server build yet.
