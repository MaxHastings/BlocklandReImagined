# Blockland ReImagined v0.1.0 (alpha)

This is the first release of Blockland ReImagined.

Blockland ReImagined is Blockland v20, rebuilt from scratch on a modern
engine. It has v20's maps, bricks, tools, weapons, vehicles, mini-games,
events, art, music and sounds. It's built for huge builds, and Add-Ons can
change almost everything. It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux, and you
don't need the original Blockland.

## What you can do

- **Build.** Use the brick selector, paint and prints, the hammer and
  wrench, and events. Brick search, the Duplicator (`/dup`) and a warning
  ghost before a plant that would fail make building easier.
- **Play.** Explore all 14 v20 maps, all 21 stock weapons and items, and
  the stock vehicles, plus the Stunt Plane. You can also play mini-games
  and fight bots.
- **Play together.** Host a LAN or Internet game, and your friends join
  straight from your IP address.
- **Go big.** Bricks are drawn in batched chunks, and joining a big build
  is quick. We're aiming for a million bricks at 60 fps.
- **Bring your old builds.** Your v20 `.bls` saves load as they are.
- **Add-Ons.** Add-Ons can add rules, HUDs, weapons, vehicles, game modes,
  creatures and whole new ways to play. Players who join download the
  Add-Ons the host runs. Old v20 Add-Ons bring their bricks, weapons and
  vehicles across.
- **Learn.** The Tutorial teaches moving, building, tools and driving, and
  the first start offers it.

It also has modern touches: autosave, crash reports, graphics presets, a
frame cap, field of view, UI size, colour-vision modes, sound captions,
and a gamepad while playing.

## Download

| System | Download |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 | `BlocklandReImagined.exe`, or the `-windows.zip` |
| macOS (Apple silicon) | the `-macos.zip` |
| Linux (x86-64) | the `-linux.zip` |

This release isn't code-signed yet, so each system warns you the first
time you open it.

**Windows.** Run `BlocklandReImagined.exe`. It sets itself up on first
start. You can also extract the zip to a folder you can write to and run
`Launch.cmd`. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**,
then **Run anyway**.

**macOS.** Unzip the download and open **BlocklandReImagined**. If macOS
says it can't check the app, click **Done**. Then go to **System Settings
> Privacy & Security**, click **Open Anyway** and confirm. After that the
app opens normally.

**Linux.** Extract the zip and run `launch.sh`. It needs glibc 2.35 or
newer (Ubuntu 22.04+, Debian 12+, Fedora, Arch, SteamOS).

## Playing together

To host, pick **LAN** or **Internet** in **Start Game**. Friends join from
**Join Game > Connect to IP**. For Internet games, the game asks your
router to open **UDP port 28000**. If it can't, forward that port to your
PC by hand. There are no accounts to make.

## It's an alpha

Expect rough edges.

- Vehicle handling is rebuilt, not copied from v20, so it may feel a
  little different.
- Bots steer simply and can get stuck on complex builds.
- Menus and building need a keyboard and mouse. A gamepad only works
  while playing.
- Scripts in old v20 Add-Ons don't run.
- The Tutorial is single player only.
- Lighting, shadows, water and sky aren't final.
- The Mac and Linux versions are the newest and have had the least play.

The full lists are in `FEATURES.md` and `KNOWN-ISSUES.md`, which come with
every download. If something breaks, `TESTER-GUIDE.md` says what to send us.
