Maxwell expanded the alpha on 2026-09-26. `alpha-contract.md` is authoritative.
There is no allowance to hand off only the Jeep, one weapon or a limited event
subset. Original content being convertible is not evidence of working gameplay.

## Reference and inventory

Use recovered stock v20 core scripts, GUIs and the stock default add-on list as
the baseline, together with original shipped content whose provenance is verified.
Keep B4v21 patches and community additions separately classified. Inventory other
verified shipped-but-disabled stock packages too. Maxwell's designated reference

## Evidence required per entry

1. Source identity and stock provenance; separate dependencies and hidden variants.
2. Conversion to native assets with stable IDs and all references resolved.
3. Native gameplay behavior, including parameters, timing, permissions and effects.
4. Reachable UI/control flow and authoritative solo/multiplayer integration.
5. Appropriate automated behavior/render checks and packaged availability, with a
   specific Maxwell playtest instruction for subjective or interactive fidelity.

Record these separately. A renderer loading a mesh does not prove its vehicle
works; an event row surviving a save does not prove the event fires. Required
vanilla entries cannot be closed as unsupported. Retain their original data while
implementing them, and keep the full handoff blocked until they work or Maxwell
explicitly changes the requirement.

## Feature families still requiring full acceptance

- Core and stock add-on bricks, hidden variants, water, checkpoint, treasure,
  pumpkin carving, teledoors, support/removal, undo/macros and destruction/respawn.
- All stock vehicle/mount types, seats, movement, weapons, collision and damage.
- All stock weapons, items, projectile types, inventories, pickup/drop and skis.
- All player configurations, avatar parts, animations, emotes, death and respawn.
- Every stock printer image/pack, print counts, selectors and persistence.
- Every stock light/emitter and original music/sound, including all attached effects.
- All brick event registrations and target classes, relays, named targets,
  delayed/cancelled/enabled events, typed parameters and effects on other systems.
- Minigame creation/settings/membership/invites, scoring, loadouts, permissions,
  lives/respawn/reset/end and corresponding UI/events/network state.
- Every verified vanilla map/environment and its special behaviors.
- Menus/settings/controls, favorites, building tools, save/load, chat commands,
  trust/player management, hosting/joining and host administration.

The alpha contract remains unchecked until evidence satisfies these families in
the actual packaged application. Use the previous UI audit as source evidence;
its statements excluding minigames/admin screens or restricting events are now
historical scope and are superseded by this contract.
