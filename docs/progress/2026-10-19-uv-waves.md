# UV waves (2026-10-19)

`UvMotion.waves` adds one periodic wave per texture axis (u, v): `pan`, `stretch`, `stretch_repeat`, `jitter`, each with
`rate` (cycles a second), `phase` (cycles) and `amplitude`. The wave runs in the vertex shader on the render clock, after
the existing centre/rotation/scroll motion's inputs, so `center` is also the stretch pivot. Material uniform grew to 10 vec4.

Formulas (pan, stretch, stretch-repeat, jitter) are the ones a UE2-style texture oscillator uses; the importer maps its enum
onto these general kinds. Jitter is uniform in [0, amplitude), redrawn at 60 a second while the cycle fraction is past
`phase` and held at the previous cycle's last value before it (the original redraws per frame; the exact random sequence is
not reproduced).

Probe: `crates/render/tests/animated_uv.rs::uv_waves_follow_the_engine_formulas` renders a 2x2 texture and checks which texel
each wave reaches at chosen times (offscreen, numeric). Played: nothing, headless only.
