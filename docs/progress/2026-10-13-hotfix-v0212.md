# 2026-10-13 Hotfix v0.2.12

Two bugs from live v0.2.11 play get a hotfix release on top of the v0.2.11
tag (main was still at 8e88129, the v0.2.11 commit), with nothing else:

- #117: the third mirror or window plane overran the sun shadow caster
  buffer (wgpu validation panic), and a refused X11 cursor grab ended the
  game at launch.
- #117 also fixes the Slayer port's `undress` erroring on an unset `dressed` flag.
- #118: player types play their own jump, pain and death sounds. This is a
  wire change (`crates/net/protocol-changes/player-type-sounds.md`), so
  v0.2.12 cannot play with v0.2.11.

## Decisions

- **v0.2.12, not v0.2.11.1.** The release workflows, `publish_release.py`
  and the in-game update check (`crates/client/src/updates.rs`) all assume
  three-part versions.
- **Integration branch, not merge PRs.** As for v0.2.10 and v0.2.11, the two
  PR heads are merged into `claude/hotfix-v0212-br7akx`, which
  `tools/release.py` gates and pushes to main; GitHub marks #117 and #118
  merged when their commits land on main.
- Nothing from v0.3.0-experimental is included.
- #118 changes `crates/addon-import/`, so `release.py` rebuilds and uploads
  the Add-On bundle, and the bundled hole bots pick up their silent jump.

## Release

On the PC: `python tools/release.py v0.2.12 --branch claude/hotfix-v0212-br7akx`.
Notes: `docs/releases/v0.2.12.md`.
