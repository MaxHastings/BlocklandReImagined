# 2026-10-27 Destruction playtest hardening

Maxwell authorized implementing the proactive three-way review, then explicitly
limited it to useful obvious fixes and a Windows playtest/remote PR. No extra
rebuild-animation feature is added; that was a later recommendation only.

The recovered Claude work is integrated with authoritative stud splits, bounded
support proofs and cosmetic client sections. The common Simulation mutation
boundary owns transient originals/descendants, exact original flags, retirement,
restoration and explicit edits committing the remaining visible geometry.
Internal same-original faces preserve intact slab continuity. Foundations use
catalog metadata, with deliberate floating roots distinct from formerly supported
roofs. Explicit damage_hidden state and timestamped world precedence cover late
join and stale cues. Selection excludes the hidden original.

Queries resume at footprint/candidate/connection-cell granularity; nearby topology
invalidates proofs, unrelated edits do not. All generated cells count against
admission (2048 per blast/tick, 16384 live transient parts). Cap/catalog exhaustion
holds the oversized original intact; it cannot amplify a rocket into deleting a
wall. Section removals/candidate changes and respawns are sliced. Collision work
is batched. Admitted ownership/round context survives shooter disconnect and is
revoked by minigame reset.

Intact client sections use a shared analytic fall/tumble and four floor probes
sharing 256 candidate steps/frame. They break into their own existing debris,
keep the original 13-second lifetime, obey Physics Quality and reject late
members after retirement. No compound collider, motion replication or crushing.
Intact weapon/player/portal interaction is intentionally absent. Non-box debris
retains its pre-existing cosmetic collision approximation. See
[design](../architecture/brick-destruction.md), [audit disposition](../audits/brick-destruction-three-way-review.md)
and [playtest card](../PLAYTEST-DESTRUCTION.md).

The broader focused run exposed the omitted trust fallback for loaded/offline
brick owners; admission now snapshots the game owner's actor and connected-owner
aliases, preserving that policy through delayed jobs. Existing LAN permissions
remain unchanged. Legacy tests for same-tick cohort respawn, an immune unsupported

The native 64-cube test also caught vertical cuts forced onto three-plate courses:
vertical midpoints prefer heights available in the piece catalog, with ordinary
brick-course cuts as fallback. This keeps native cubes coarse away from the bite
and avoids splitting unsupported five-plate heights into excessive tiny pieces.

The native case also exposed a more fundamental recovered-code mismatch: normal
converted plastic faces have a source-backed 0.0012 render rim, outside the plain
box detector's 0.001 tolerance. Classification now admits that render skin while
keeping exact one-box collision checks. The stock category also includes tall
Cubes: foundation protection additionally requires a one-plate slab, so a ground
64-cube remains breakable as Maxwell requested. No cube-name exception is used.
