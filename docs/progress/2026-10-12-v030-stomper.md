# 2026-10-12 v0.3.0: the Stomper, a simple enemy on the shared bot brain

Max asked whether a Goomba-style Add-On can make its NPC "really simple and
boring", not as sophisticated as the Blockhead Bot, on the one shared brain.
The Playground now proves it with an original stand-in.

## What changed

- `packages/playground/playground-stomper` (archetype and bot kind) and
  `playground-stomper-look` (its box model, client side): a squat walking
  mushroom 1.2 units tall with 40 health and no jet. Its bot kind is the
  shared brain with `arm`, `carry`, `interact`, `objective`, `search` and
  `fly` weighted 0, surprise, teamwork and perception strength 0, its own
  side, and a `melee` stomp. Both packages are listed in
  `default-addons.json` turned off, and the Playground mode names them.
- One new bot-kind field, `weapons` (default true). With `false`, a kind
  never takes up, draws or fires a hand weapon and puts away any the rules
  hand it, so it fights with its `melee` or a vehicle gun it crews. Without
  it, the mode's Carbine loadout armed the Stomper, which then shot instead
  of stomping. Default kinds are unchanged. Documented in
  `docs/architecture/bots.md` (Data).
- `crates/sim/tests/playground.rs`:
  `a_stomper_walks_up_and_stomps_a_runner_with_its_body`. Two Stompers from
  spawn bricks play in the Stomper body, keep their hands empty all game,
  never hurt each other, and one stomps the Runner.
- The network Playground test now expects clients to download the Stomper
  and its look.
