## Changes and review

The release combines Rule Workshop conditions, variables, scoring, regions and
nine editable recipes; shared bot ground-vehicle crews, dated target evidence,
safe charge cancellation and physical pushing; background Add-On preparation
and reloads; and lighting, paint, door, geometry-identity and spawn-heading fixes.
The credited bundle retains all 87 pinned original community Add-Ons.

Maxwell's required usability correction is included: **Detection region** in
the Wrench directly authors dimensions, previews edits and saves with Send.
Cancel discards the draft. Building tools reveal region outlines; putting them
away hides the outlines. Detection and presentation share the same bounds
calculation. Static authoring requires no setup event or activation. Runtime
`setRegionSize` remains available for rules. See the
[authoring/release review](2026-10-02-region-authoring-release-review.md).

That review also fixed unavailable optional starting equipment in Workshop
creation, Add-Ons folder failure feedback and obsolete bot descriptions.
The audit includes offscreen original/synthetic rendering, save/trust/atomicity
checks and the Windows fixture failures and corrections. Required assertions
and the per-binary watchdog remain in place.

## Interactive reports and next work

During ongoing Maxwell-only playtesting, newer reports in the Workshop thread
identified issues now being addressed on its **v0.2.1-playtest** branch:

- Recipe brick selection can choose the Zombie bot-hole as an ordinary control
  when that Add-On is enabled, spawning bots across unrelated examples.
- A stale map-list reply can leave host Admin controls waiting indefinitely.
- Map changes do not initialize some Add-On player defaults, affecting
  Duplicator clicks.
- Third-person portal traversal can flicker or collide with a working opening.
- Detection-region and event-row controls need more spacing/style refinement;
  bot jet pursuit needs better close-range braking and altitude handling.

The owning thread reports reproduced causes and initial regressions; these
fixes are **not part of v0.2.0** and their combined validation remains its work.
The public release notes explicitly list the new open playtest reports. The
released tag/assets remain immutable, and active follow-up worktrees are
preserved. These findings are evidence that automated checks do not certify
complete creator usability or subjective feel.

The private saves corpus is unavailable and remains explicitly **uncovered**;
the gate reported that skip. Bot aircraft/boat piloting, strategic objectives,
construction and full ballistic/portal trajectory planning remain future work.

enter/leave event; the nine recipes (mind the known Zombie selection issue);
Add-On changes while hosting/joining, map changes, night lighting, paint and
doors; allied ground crews, blocked routes, a human driver, team changes while
