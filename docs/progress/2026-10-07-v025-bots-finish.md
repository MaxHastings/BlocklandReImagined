# 2026-10-07 v0.2.5 bots: the finish plan

Branch `claude/v0.2.5-s4s5bn`, worked through `v0.2.5/finish-plan.md`
(sections 3A to 3D) and Max's two playtest items.

## What changed

- **An enemy is worth fighting only if the bot can hurt it** (C1). At play,
  `bot_menace` scores Fight and Chase at 0 for a bot with no attack, and
  `HURT` (1.25, above the play) for an enemy that hurt it. With nothing at
  stake an unarmed bot still closes in. This retired the three objective
  stopgaps (pausing delivery when hurt, the peaceful-objective arming
  gate, the filter that hid enemies).
- **Every sight scan on the shared ray budget** (C2, rule 6): explore's look
  round (and a failed one is remembered: no rescan until it has moved 4
  units or 2 s pass), the Gravity Gun throw's open-spot search (one
  candidate at a time, remembered; it used to rescan every tick while an
  objective held the body, about 850 rays a tick), the goof's player in
  sight and the idle mark (`bot_sees_player`).
- **The act stage owns aim, trigger and the stall jump** (A2, C3, C6). Looks
  are proposals (`act::Looker`) like walks; the trigger has one owner a
  tick in `bot_act`; the stall judge runs before the stage resolves and
  its jump is a press. `act::Acted` is the F3 readout's fifth line ("Acts:
  walk route, look target, trigger fight; held at an edge; stalled 1,
  replanned 0").
- **Goofs end through the chooser** (A3, B2): a goof goes on while the
  play-or-goof choice still picks it, held by the hold rule its first two
  seconds or so; playing's boredom is relieved as it ends. The door goof
  holds until its click lands. The detour walks to an explore spot at most
  8 units off.
- **One of each mechanism** (B6, B9 to B14): the strafe's own air guard went
  (the act stage's edge check covers it); one weapon reader (an unknown
  scripted item is no weapon to the bot rather than a guessed melee
  swing); failed objective steps are a `Cooldowns`; the two `RETRY`
  constants are named for what they do; the reflex random stream went (a
  cadence salt instead); budget numbers come from `objectives::limits()`;
  `BotHold.margin` and `ObjectStamp.spawner_name` went; `Situation.gunning`
  went (it scored the same as without).
- **A trapped bot respawns itself** (Max's playtest item 1): a Respawn
  behaviour scored from the stuck evidence (trying to get somewhere and
  staying within 2 units for over 10 s), sure 10 s later, never with an
  enemy in sight, when hurt or while carrying. It gives the player's
  Ctrl+K command (`Command::Suicide`).
- **Hammer in the face** (Max's playtest item 2): the tool goof that walks
  up to someone takes out an item whose image strikes up close (`melee` in
  its data) when it has one; a tool that can hurt is still used only on
  someone the rules let it hurt no one.
- **A search looks one way and back** all round, easing in and out of each
  point, instead of a steady spin (D1).

## Queued for v0.2.6, each with why

- Idle ball play on the one shared push: it works again (below), but
  still runs its own push beside the objective's.
- The Gravity Gun throw on the objective's hold: the throw is a mini
  objective (carry the grip to open sky, then swing), more than folding
  two controllers.
- One progress judge per objective step: the claim lease (3 s, frees a
  contested body for a teammate) and the step deadline (30 s, gives up the
  step) judge on different clocks; folding them changes who gets the ball.
- Commitment from the hold rule instead of the committed score (0.92):
  needs a hold that outlasts a fight, which changes CTF delivery.
- The 13 test-only kind numbers as constants: tests use them to make
  fights deterministic (no reaction delay, instant turns), so each test
  needs rewriting first.
- Strolls weighed by the explore scorer: picking among several spots
  sent a swimmer out of its water and a guard out of its room (the old
  single random spot keeps them in); needs leash-aware scoring first.
- The spray can is still told by its image id in two places (the goof's
  hold-down, the team's spray check); by its data instead needs a
  "hold to use" field the can does not carry yet.
- Emotes from the bot's own signals, hit-while-strafing turns, and the
  first-come line-of-fire rule (F9) and aim by target size (P5).

## Final review fixes (8eee79c7)

An independent review of ba8af8cd found eight things a player could hit,
all in code this branch changed; each is fixed at its cause:

1. Bots go through the packages' rules as players do (`package_policy`
   no longer skips bots): a game with suicide off stops a bot's Respawn.
2. A life a bot ended itself begins marked; within 4 units of where that
   respawn brought it, Respawn scores 0, so it cannot loop.
3. A mounted bot never respawns itself.
4. A carrier hurt by a new attacker keeps delivering (`HURT` applies only
   when the play is not being carried home).
5. Idle play's leash is the chase radius while it works a body, so it no
   longer walks home mid-push; the idle ball test passes.
6. A push-only attack (the broom) counts as an attack, and an opposing
   player in a game is a target for a bot that can shove; the soccer test
   requires broom kits to shove.
7. `has_possible_attack` counts an unknown mechanism only when
   `Session::image_weapon`, the reader the bot fires by, can use it.
8. The hammer goof takes out a close striker only if it cannot hurt that
   person.

Also the dead "clear" callout term, three more sight rays onto the shared
budget, and the Acts line naming the mover even when it stands still.
A test of a bot boxed in at its own spawn was tried and dropped: the
spawn put the bot outside the box, so it did not test the loop.
