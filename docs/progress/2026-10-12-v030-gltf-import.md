# 2026-10-12 v0.3.0 W5: glTF/GLB models into native shapes

Branch `claude/gltf-importer-boc77k`, into `v0.3.0-experimental`. W5 of the
v0.3.0 plan: Add-On authors bring characters, weapons and vehicles from
Blender without hand-building models. Max wants Mario, Master Chief,
Goombas, a Warthog and Halo weapons ready to drop in when v0.3.0 lands.

## What changed

- **`bri-import-addon gltf MODEL OUT --kind body|item|prop`**
  (`crates/addon-import/src/gltf/`). A reader for `.glb` and `.gltf` (buffers,
  data URIs, sparse accessors, every component type; no new dependency) and
  a converter to `bri_content::shape::Shape`:
  - glTF faces +Z, the game -Z: half a turn about Y for points, rotations
    and inverse bind matrices. Native nodes carry no scale, so each node's
    even scale moves into its children's translations, its rigid meshes and
    its joints' inverse binds; an uneven scale stays exact on a leaf mesh and
    is warned about elsewhere. `--scale` and `--height` rescale everything,
    clips included.
  - Skinning (4 weights per vertex, normalized; unweighted vertices are
    named), rigid meshes on bones, triangle strips and fans, flat normals
    when a mesh has none, `col_` meshes into a collision detail.
  - Materials: the Base Color texture (PNG or JPEG) times its factor, or a
    swatch of the factor, as `<material>.png`; textures over 2048 are scaled;
    wrap, alpha blend and unlit carried; other maps named as ignored.
  - Clips sampled at `--fps` (30): linear, step and cubic spline. Clips named
    after the W2 animation roles (`idle`, `run`, `aim_right`, `sit`, `look`
    and the rest, or the Blockhead's own names) are stored under the
    Blockhead's clip names, so a body needs no `animations` map and works
    whether or not the W2 role map is present. Loops loop, actions play once,
    `look`/`head_side`/`head_up` become additive overlays relative to their
    level frame (as the Blockhead's are). Channels left at rest are dropped
    so an aim or look layer does not freeze the body under it. A loop that
    walks its root away is warned about.
  - Body sockets: `socket_right_hand`, `socket_left_hand` and `socket_eye`
    empties (or a hand bone, or `--right-hand NODE`) get `Mount0`, `Mount1`
    and `Eye` children turned level with the body in the rest pose, the
    Blockhead names every body falls back to. Items level their
    `mountPoint`; props list `mount`/`hub` positions and collision hull
    corners for `vehicles.json`.
  - Output: a body is a whole Add-On (manifest, `content.json`, archetype
    with the model); an item or prop is a `models/` folder. Every run writes
    `IMPORT-REPORT.md` (or `--json`): sockets, clips and how they play, each
    missing role and what stands in, warnings with the fix.
- **Imported bodies draw their own textures** (`crates/client/src/avatar.rs`
  `body_mesh`): an Add-On body's objects named as a Blockhead part or colour
  slot are painted or hidden as before (the Shark's head and chest painted,
  its helmet hidden); every other object now draws untinted instead of being
  hidden, which hid any model not built on Blockhead part names.
- Docs: `docs/modding/gltf-models.md` (the authoring contract Max prepares
  models against; also in the project files as `v0.3.0/gltf-authoring.md`),
  the modding index, two interop matrix rows.

## Evidence

- `cargo test -p bri-addon-import --lib gltf`: 12 pass. The test figure is
  original (`gltf/testing.rs` writes it): a scaled armature, skinned body,
  rigid crown with uneven scale, an angled hand-socket empty, textures and
  nine clips. Posed clips are compared with the same vertex posed the way a
  glTF viewer computes it (`clips_play_as_a_gltf_viewer_shows_them`,
  `height_scales_the_whole_figure_and_its_clips`); the written package passes
  `bri_package_runtime::check`.
- `cargo test -p bri-client --test gltf_body`: the imported package loads
  through `AvatarAssets::load_bodies`, every triangle draws untinted at 1.6
  units, `Mount0` is at the hand, level, swings with `run` and drops when
  seated; an offscreen render (lavapipe) shows its own texture.
- `cargo test -p bri-client --lib avatar`: 28 pass (the Shark-style fish
  body still paints its parts and hides unselected accessories).
- Khronos sample models (not committed) through the command: CesiumMan,
  Fox, RiggedFigure and BrainStem all convert and validate at `--height 2.6`;
  their reports name the missing sockets and roles.

## Not verified here

- Two networked clients drawing an imported body (the W5 "done when"): the
  Playground network test is the place; not added yet.
- Before W2 (#109) merges, a body must have `idle`, `run`, `back`, `side`,
  `crouch`, `look` and `head_side` clips to load; with W2 any subset works.
- No model was exported from a real Blender session in this container; the
  Khronos samples stand in for exporter output.
