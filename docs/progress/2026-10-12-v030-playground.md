# 2026-10-12 v0.3.0: Mixed Add-On Playground and interop matrix

First workstream of v0.3.0 (seamless Add-On composition). The plan lives in
the project files (`v0.3.0/plan.md`); this entry covers W1.

## What changed

- `packages/playground/`: five original Add-Ons (Runner body and look, Pulse
  Carbine, Dune Buggy, and a mode that plays them with `blockhead_bot`),
  listed in `default-addons.json` turned off. Art and sounds come from
  `tools/make_playground_assets.py` (stdlib, deterministic).
- `crates/sim/tests/playground.rs`: six single-server tests, one per
  combination (stand alone, join and fire, drive through a fort, passenger
  fires, respawn keeps body and loadout, bot fights Runners).
- `crates/net/tests/playground.rs`: two clients and a clean-cache late
  joiner agree on bodies, the weapon in hand, the driven buggy and the
  knocked-out bricks.
- `docs/audits/interop-matrix.md`: what works, what was fixed, what is a
  gap and which workstream owns it.

Two generic engine defects found and fixed (no Add-On is named):

- A spawn brick's bot joined its builder's mini-game, so in a game mode
  (whose mini-game holds everyone) it ended up outside the game and could
  not fight. `spawn_brick_game` now returns the mode's server game first.
- A vehicle smashing bricks was credited to its owner (or whoever last
  pushed it), not its driver; a driver of someone else's vehicle had no
  permission and nothing broke. `vehicle_struck` credits the driver first,
  as run-overs already did.

## Evidence

- `cargo test -p bri-sim --test playground`: 6 passed.
- `cargo test -p bri-net --test playground`: passed 6 runs in a row after
  the fixes below.
- `python tools/check.py focused`: see the PR.

## Failures on the way

- The network test first failed intermittently because the watcher client
  was not read while the driver drove, so its socket dropped vehicle
  datagrams. The test now keeps every client reading, like a real one, and
  asserts what clients must agree on rather than an exact real-time path.
- A bot that respawns on a player's spawn point freezes. Handed to the
  thread "Bot frozen when spawning on a player"; the test uses two spawn
  points meanwhile.
- The buggy drifts left when driven straight and bounces when boarded.
  Recorded in the matrix for a vehicle-feel pass.

## Next

W2 (sockets, animation roles with fallbacks, muzzle from the weapon, eyes
