# Sky takes the world's fog

Max's report (2026-10-01): with Thick Fog, far terrain and buildings faded to
grey but the sky stayed a clear blue, so fogged mountains showed as pale
cut-outs against it.

Cause: only geometry was fogged. The sky faces and clouds were drawn unfogged;
the only sky fog was a fixed 60-unit horizon band, built once from the map's
own fog end and only on maps whose Sky draws bans. Live fog (the environment
presets) never reached the sky.

Fix: one fog definition, `crates/render/src/fog.wgsl` (prepended to every
shader by `color::shader_source`, CPU twin `bri_content::environment::Fog`).
`fog_at` is the world curve; `sky_fog_at` fogs a sky ray as if the sky stood
where the ray climbs `SKY_FOG_CEILING` (60 units, the old band's height) above
the eye. At and below the horizon that is past the fog end, so the sky is the
fog colour exactly where far geometry is; thin fog leaves a horizon haze and
thick fog reaches far up the sky, like v20's fog bans. Sky faces, clouds, the
mirror pass and the world share it; the separate band geometry is gone.

Guard: `persistent_scene::sky_fades_into_the_world_fog_at_the_horizon`
(horizon sky = fog colour, a mid ray matches `Fog::sky_amount`, zenith clear
under far fog); it fails on the old shader (horizon stayed the face colour).
Cost: one fog evaluation per sky pixel, 16 fewer band quads.
