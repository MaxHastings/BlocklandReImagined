# One atmosphere for everything far away

Max's report (2026-10-01): with Thick Fog, far terrain and buildings faded to
grey but the sky stayed a clear blue, so fogged mountains showed as pale
cut-outs. Max then asked for modern practice rather than v20 exactness for
fog, sky and the far horizon, with map settings still driving the look.

Cause: only geometry was fogged. Sky faces, clouds and particles were drawn
unfogged; the only sky fog was a fixed 60-unit horizon band, built once from
the map's own fog end and only on maps whose Sky draws bans. Live fog (the
environment presets) never reached the sky.

Now one definition, `crates/render/src/fog.wgsl`, prepended to every shader
by `color::shader_source`; its CPU twin `bri_content::environment::Fog`
documents it and its constants are injected from there:

1. Exponential fog. Level with the eye it starts at the fog distance and
   reaches optical depth `FOG_DEPTH` (4, 98%) at the visible distance.
2. Height falloff. Above the eye the fog thins by e every `FOG_HEIGHT` (60)
   units, so the sky is the fog colour at the horizon and clearer overhead:
   thin fog is a horizon haze, thick fog covers most of the sky. Below the
   eye it stays level-density, so the map's visible distance still holds.
3. Soft edge. Over the last quarter of the range geometry fades to the
   sky's own fog in its direction, so the world ends exactly as fogged as
   the sky behind it: no silhouettes cut out where drawing stops.

Users: world, terrain, water, bricks, players and vehicles (scene.wgsl), sky
faces and clouds, mirrors, and particles (blended sprites take the fog
colour, added light fades; `EffectsRenderer::set_fog`). The band geometry is
gone. Unfogged on purpose: Add-On boxes and outlines (overlays). Live
shadows already fade over the last tenth of their range and are fogged with
the surface they land on. Foliage keeps its own distance fade.

Guards: `persistent_scene::sky_fades_into_the_world_fog_at_the_horizon`
(horizon sky is fog, a mid ray and the zenith match `Fog::sky_amount`,
geometry at the end equals the sky's fog), `sky_orientation_...` (geometry
matches `Fog::amount`), `gpu_contract` fogged sprite cases. Cost: a few
exp() per fogged pixel, no new passes.

## 2026-10-02: Skylands' floor came back

Max: Skylands lost its floor in v0.1.12 (pale fog below the horizon). Its
floor is the sky's bottom face, the mirrored sky; the fog took everything
below the horizon as the plain fog backdrop, so that face was fully fogged.
`SceneData::sky_below` (the sky has a bottom face) now reaches the shaders as
`Camera::fog_color.w`: with it the fog is symmetric about the eye, so the
floor hazes toward the horizon and is clear straight down, and far geometry
below the eye fades to that floor's own fog. Without it nothing changes.
Guard: the `NEG_Y` case of `sky_fades_into_the_world_fog_at_the_horizon`
(white fog on main, the floor's colour now).
