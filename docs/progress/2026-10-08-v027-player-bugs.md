# 2026-10-08 v0.2.7 player bugs: teamkills, Close Quarters, far fog

Branch `claude/v027-player-bugs-ixebvx`, on main 9019e8d.

## Rare bot teamkills (gauntlet capture_the_flag, zombie_survival)
- **Cause (reproduced on the PC):** a shot at an enemy with a teammate
  standing on past it. The harm a plan prices (`harm::shot_harm`) ended at
  the first body its way met, the target, and the way itself ended half a
  unit past the target (`combat::clear_path`), so a teammate behind came
  out as ally harm 0. The target stepped aside, or the shot went wide by
  the aim error, and the bullet flew on. Diagnostics on the PC (debug, like
  the gate): shooter and victim grounded on every tick, no leap or stall
  hop, planned `{enemy 25, ally 0}`, victim 15-30 units past the target near
  the line. The earlier guess (nav leaps not in the ally prediction) was
  wrong.
- **Fix:** a straight shot (a ray, or a projectile that does not fall)
  also prices what it does flying on past its way's end out to its reach,
  missing the bodies its way met (`harm::past`, `harm::overshoot`, called
  from `combat::assess` for the aimed and the aim-error way). Only its own
  side's harm counts. The way past comes in 4-unit pieces timed by the
  projectile's speed, so a teammate is met where it will have walked to. A
  wall cuts it, checked with one world sweep only when a teammate is on
  that line. Falling arcs and throws are unchanged.
- **Test:** `harm::tests::a_shot_that_misses_its_target_flies_on_into_a_teammate_behind_it`.
- **Evidence (PC, debug, 8c08ee3):** capture_the_flag kills 29, team_kills
  0, team_damage 0 (before: 25, 1, 23); zombie_survival kills 35,
  team_kills 0, team_damage 0 (before: 33, 1, 75); all_dials_on passes. Whole
  bot_gauntlet 21 passed; bot_firefight, bot_tactics, bot_interactions,
  acceptance_unfamiliar, bot_battle_perf pass. Cloud (Linux): bri-sim and
  the same chaos targets pass.
- The three gate-known-failures entries and the whole-match
  `team_kills == 0` asserts are left to the tests thread, which owns them.
- **Not fixed, seen in all_dials_on:** in a_jeep_on_each_side a driving bot
  runs over its own teammate (bot3 to bot4 twice, bot6 to bot5 once, all
  killed). It is the driver's chase, not a shot plan, so it is outside
  this fix; it does not fail any assertion.

## Close Quarters with HE Grenade off
- A fresh install's default Add-On choices leave HE Grenade off, and the
  Close Quarters save stocks HE Grenades, so the dedicated server refused
  it ("Missing authored item bounds").
- A static item the server has no bounds for now spawns nothing
  (`ItemSpawners::reconcile`, `validate_append`); the brick keeps naming
  it, so it comes back when the Add-On is on, and a wrench Send leaving
  that item alone passes.
- Test: `items::a_saved_item_whose_add_on_is_off_is_left_out_and_the_world_still_loads`
  (fails before the change). Not checked against real content here: no
  generated content in the cloud.

## Distant fog toward the sky's colour
- Geometry fogged toward the flat fog colour, so tall far terrain stood
  pale against the clearer sky above the horizon (review-fog-sky.md).
- Geometry now fogs toward the sky behind it: the authored sky faces'
  average colour per direction band (8 around, 16 up, finest at the
  horizon; `SceneData::sky_bands`, computed once in
  `environment_scene::append`, carried in `Camera::sky_bands`), tinted and
  under the fog of the world's edge, as the sky pass draws it. Level and
  low it is the fog colour as before; high up it is the sky's own, so the
  far fade meets the sky it dithers into. Clouds and the sun flare are
  left out of the target. Mirrors and particles are unchanged.
- Tests (lavapipe in the cloud): `persistent_scene::tall_far_geometry_fogs_toward_the_sky_behind_it`
  (fails on the old shader: grey 198 where the sky gives 199/117/117),
  `environment_scene::tests::sky_bands_hold_the_face_each_way_and_leave_the_backdrop_out`;
  `sky_orientation_translation_depth_and_distance_fog` updated for the
  new target (the -Z face's 2% shows at the horizon).
- For Max's playtest: a tall-terrain map under default fog and Thick Fog,
  and Skylands looking down. The far-edge stipple crawl is unchanged.
