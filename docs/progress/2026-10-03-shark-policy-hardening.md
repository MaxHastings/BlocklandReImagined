## Implemented package policy

- Deterministic five-position palette and buoyancy-archetype selection, named
  or 1-in-51 Cool Shark helmet/visor, white fins, and one initialization per
  living actor identity. Dead/removed IDs are pruned from bounded state.
- Its own hole disables rendering, collision and raycasting after plant/load.
- Eligible native Bite damage at observed body contact may mount a victim on
  the authored mouth node. White sharks always attempt capture; others retain
  the one-in-three decision and ordinary 35-damage fallback. Sandbox,
  cross-game, already-mounted and unsupported-body captures are denied.
- Capture tracks actual holder/victim/mount/game identity, rests the holder,
  changes the human camera through the existing orbit operation, and uses
  ordinary attributed damage after 600 simulation ticks. It releases before
  lethal damage so canonical death cleanup cannot make unmount stale.
- Death, spawn, leave and MiniGame reset/end/configuration/team hooks release
  captures. Per-tick observation also releases on missing/dead actors, changed
  body/game/mount or lost damage permission. The first 24 capture ticks retain
  the source's short harm grace; large hits or the one-in-four decision release
  afterward. Release restores camera and wakes the authorized holder.
