# 2026-10-15 Fog haze: per-map fog height and a cover ceiling

For a Blood Gulch-like map (an imported map Add-On in a private repo; no
Halo content here): far canyon cliffs should soften into haze but stay
visible, with thicker haze low down than up high. Branch `visuals-gloss-fog`.
A glossy/glass model material was started on the same branch and dropped
at Max's request (existing materials cover the windshield and hull); none
of it is committed.

## What changed

- `bri_content::environment::Fog` gains `height` (units per e-fold,
  default `FOG_HEIGHT` 60) and `max` (most cover, above 0 to 1, default 1).
  Both are left out of serialized files at their defaults, so existing packs
  keep their bytes and digests. With `max < 1` (`Fog::haze`) the fog never
  covers more than `max` of geometry or sky, and geometry no longer dithers
  out over the last quarter of the range: it stops at `end`.
- Shaders: `fog.wgsl`'s functions take `h` (`Fog::haze_uniform`: height,
  max). Every fog user's uniform gets a `haze` vec4 at its end: the scene
  `Camera` (both `scene.wgsl` and `scene_original.wgsl`), the mirror
  `FrameUniform`, the AO `Uniform` and fx-runtime's `GpuCamera`
  (`set_fog` takes it). `atmosphere.z` stays the animation time. A zero
  `haze` reads as the standard fog, so a pass that never sets it is unchanged.
- Overrides: `atmosphere::Settings::fog_height` / `fog_max` (validated,
  merged, unset and taken like the others; `Live` carries them as
  `Option`, applied by `Camera::apply_atmosphere`) and Rhai
  `set_environment` keys `fog_height`, `fog_max` (read back by
  `environment()`). Wire change: `protocol-changes/environment-fog-haze.md`.
- Map Add-Ons: optional `fog` on the bundle's `maps` entry
  (`{start, end, color, height?, max?}`), applied by `scene_loader` (the
  backdrop is the fog colour when the map has no sky) and checked by
  `content::map_index` (so `bri-addon-check` reports a bad one).
- Docs: `docs/modding/rules.md` (keys), `docs/modding/maps.md` ("Fog and
  haze"), `docs/architecture/map-packages.md`.

## Evidence

- `cargo test -p bri-content --lib`: `unset_haze_reads_as_the_fog_before_it`
  (defaults match the old formula exactly), `a_haze_never_covers_everything_and_stops_at_the_far_plane`,
  `a_lower_fog_height_keeps_the_haze_near_the_ground`,
  `fog_height_and_max_override_the_map_only_when_set`.
- `cargo test -p bri-render --lib --test shader_validation --test map_fog
  --test persistent_scene` on lavapipe: all pass, including the new GPU
  twin test `a_haze_caps_the_fog_and_keeps_far_geometry` and the existing
  default-fog twin tests.
- `cargo test -p bri-package-runtime --lib` (`a_map_may_give_its_own_fog`).
- `cargo clippy -p bri-render -p bri-client -p bri-fx-runtime -p bri-content
  -p bri-package-runtime --tests -- -D warnings`, and earlier with
  bri-convert and bri-addon-import: clean. `cargo fmt --check` clean.
- Not run: the full gate, `focused` (too many dependents of bri-content for
  this 4-core machine and its nearly full disk).
