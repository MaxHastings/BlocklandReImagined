# 2026-10-07 Ally harm: one mechanism for what a shot does to its side

Bots used several checks to keep from hurting their own side, each with its
own margins:
- an ally cone (`clear_cone`, `SPREAD_CLEAR`)
- `bot_fire_clear`
- the miss check (`bot_miss_spares_allies`)
- `safe_blast` with its clearances
- `clear_path`'s ally loop
- `BLAST_CLEARANCE` in the standoff

They are replaced by one mechanism, `harm::shot_harm`. It returns what the
shot would do, in health, to enemies, allies and the shooter. It reuses the
chords that check the shot reaches its target, and it judges the path with
the bot's aim error applied.

- A ray goes to its reach. A projectile goes chord by chord. A timed throw
  follows its bounces to the burst. A swing goes to the body the hammer finds.
- The first body on the way ends it. Bodies are grown by their motion.
- Pellets share out v20's per-axis spread box, and nearer bodies shade
  farther ones.
- The blast uses the host's own falloff, now exported as
  `runtime::blast_falloff`.
- A body counts only where the rules let the shooter hurt it: friendly fire,
  self damage, radius damage and spawn protection. A body never counts for
  more than its health left. Its own shot ends the shooter's protection. An
  enemy's protection is waited out by the fire gate.
- The trade is Max's decision ("Trade it"). Worth is enemy harm minus ally
  harm minus own harm, one for one. A shot is no option when that is not
  positive, when it would take the shooter's health left, or when it would
  kill a teammate.
- The trigger keeps the shot's swept shape with the plan. It fires only
  while no body that wasn't counted in the plan has stepped into that shape.
  The fire gate judges the actual shot again.

## Changed asserts

- `bot_gauntlet`: `deathmatch_open_field`, `deathmatch_mixed_arsenal`,
  `rooftop_brawl_without_rails`, `zombie_survival` and `capture_the_flag`
  asserted `team_kills + at_ally == 0`. They now assert `team_kills == 0`
  and that a side's damage to its own is less than its damage to enemies,
  read from `Session::damage_results`. `sane` asserts no planned shot hurts
  its side as much as its enemies or kills a teammate. The new
  `no_planned_shot_trades_its_side_for_less_over_seeds` runs the mixed
  arsenal over 3 seeds.
- `bot_tactics`:
  - `nearby_blast_risk_selects_the_safe_second_inventory_slot` asserted the
    old 7-unit blast clearance. It is now
    `a_close_blast_weapon_is_fired_only_as_a_trade_its_holder_wins`: the bot
    hurts its enemy, hurts itself less than that, and lives.
  - The low-roof test asserts that the bot is never hurt by its own blast,
    in place of the 7-unit clearance.
  - `replacing_the_charged_equipment_cancels_without_throwing` counts
    recovery as the replacement weapon firing, not as a hit, which depends
    on aim luck.
- `bot_perception::first_wound` turns goofs off. A gesture on sight held
  fire for 230 ticks, whatever the bot's reaction.
