# 2026-10-07 Ally harm: one mechanism for what a shot does to its side

Bots used several checks to keep from hurting their own side, each with its
own margins:
- an ally cone (`clear_cone`, `SPREAD_CLEAR`)
- `bot_fire_clear`
- the miss check (`bot_miss_spares_allies`)
- `safe_blast` with its clearances
- `clear_path`'s ally loop
- `BLAST_CLEARANCE` in the standoff

They are replaced by one mechanism, `harm::shot_harm`. It returns what the
shot would do, in health, to enemies, allies and the shooter. It reuses the
chords that check the shot reaches its target, and it judges the path with
the bot's aim error applied.

- A ray goes to its reach. A projectile goes chord by chord. A timed throw
  follows its bounces to the burst. A swing goes to the body the hammer finds.
- The first body on the way ends it. Bodies are grown by their motion.
- Pellets share out v20's per-axis spread box, and nearer bodies shade
  farther ones.
- The blast uses the host's own falloff, now exported as
  `runtime::blast_falloff`.
- A body counts only where the rules let the shooter hurt it: friendly fire,
  self damage, radius damage and spawn protection. A body never counts for
  more than its health left. Its own shot ends the shooter's protection. An
  enemy's protection is waited out by the fire gate.
- The trade is Max's decision ("Trade it"). Worth is enemy harm minus ally
  harm minus own harm, one for one. A shot is no option when that is not
  positive, when it would take the shooter's health left, or when it would
  kill a teammate.
- The trigger keeps the shot's swept shape with the plan. It fires only
  while no body that wasn't counted in the plan has stepped into that shape.
  The fire gate judges the actual shot again.

## Changed asserts

- `bot_gauntlet`: `deathmatch_open_field`, `deathmatch_mixed_arsenal`,
  `rooftop_brawl_without_rails`, `zombie_survival` and `capture_the_flag`
  asserted `team_kills + at_ally == 0`. They now assert `team_kills == 0`

  its side as much as its enemies or kills a teammate. The new

  - `nearby_blast_risk_selects_the_safe_second_inventory_slot` asserted the

    in place of the 7-unit clearance.
  - `replacing_the_charged_equipment_cancels_without_throwing` counts
    recovery as the replacement weapon firing, not as a hit, which depends
    on aim luck.
- `bot_perception::first_wound` turns goofs off. A gesture on sight held
  fire for 230 ticks, whatever the bot's reaction.

## Mounted guns (commit 2)

A gunner's shot now goes through the same chooser. `mounted_gun` reads the
seat's gun as a `Capability` (`tactics::vehicle_capability`) through the
same projectile reader as hand weapons (`projectile_attack`). The shot is
solved from the muzzle with the vehicle's motion, its path is checked, and
its harm is priced by `shot_harm`. Same-vehicle crew don't stop its path,
but the blast still counts against them. A gun seat fires only when the
chooser says `Ready`. The mount's `Unsupported` is gone: a passenger off
the gun seat fights with its hand weapons through the ordinary plan. The
charged-gun release (hold until charged, then let go) stays, because it is
how the gun fires, not a safety rule.

New test: `bot_interactions::a_gunner_never_fires_a_blast_that_hurts_its_crew_or_an_ally`.
A rover with a rocket gun and a crew of bots fires on its target, hurts it,
and never hurts or plans to hurt its own side.

## Review and acceptance-run follow-up

- Review must-fixes: the gauntlet checks each side's damage to its own side
  separately (`Report::damage_by_side`), and planned shots count ally plus
  own harm, as the fire gate does. Dead code is gone, and the reaction test
  runs with goofs on again.
- No pushes fired after ally harm. The fire gate left the push out of the
  harm it compared, and the trigger only priced bodies that took damage.
  Fixed: `Harm::push`, and every body the shot meets is now priced.
  `push_brooms_on_a_high_deck` covers it.
- Shoves off long drops are worth the speed the target falls at by then,
  not 0.
- Own-side grenade harm had two causes: a thrower walked back over its
  own resting canister, and a throw that bounced off a body landed by the
  thrower. Fixed: a throw that would bounce back is priced as if it lands
  by the thrower, and bots stay out of live blasts or walk out of them.
- Broom soccer stalled once pushes fired. A contest push is now worth its
  value only against a claimant at the ball.
- Acceptance run: pushes off the drop are reported, not asserted. No bot
  planned a push with worth in any run. Lining up shoves is v0.2.7.
- The `team_kills=1` once seen in `weapons_lying_on_the_ground` no longer
  reproduces: 0 on the current tree, traced with a hook on every team kill.

Evidence: all `bri-sim` tests pass. All `bri-chaos` tests pass (46
binaries). `all_dials_on` passes. Release `bot_think_time_16`: 764 us/tick.

## Second review

- The way out of a live blast was applied after the safety stage's other
  checks and was never checked itself. It is now decided first, so the
  portal, vehicle and edge checks apply to it. Test:
  `the_way_out_of_a_blast_meets_the_edge_check`.
- One trade rule, `tactics::trade`, now serves the chooser, the fire gate
  and the gauntlet. `harm_allows` is gone.
- The bot readout's planned harm was always `None` after a step: bots plan
  before the tick advances. It now shows the plan from the step that led
  here. With plans visible, the gauntlet flagged just-killed bots, and a
  shot that does no harm to its shooter no longer counts as killing it.
- The push proof is now played through in the shove unit test: the bot
  plans the push, fires it through its ordinary controls, and the target
  goes off the edge. `push_brooms_on_a_high_deck` (three seeds) asserts
  that planned pushes with worth are fired, and prints how many knock an
  enemy off.
- Windows and Linux diverge on the same seed. Likely cause, inferred and
  not proven: our own `sim` and `motor` code calls `f32` `sin`, `cos`,
  `atan2`, `powf` and `exp` from `std`. Those use the platform C library
  (MSVC's on Windows, glibc on Linux), which can differ in the last bit,
  and the bots' chaotic feedback amplifies that. Rapier already has
  `enhanced-determinism` on. Fix for v0.2.7: route these through `libm`.

Evidence: all `bri-sim` tests pass. All `bri-chaos` tests pass (46
binaries). `all_dials_on` passes. Release `bot_think_time_16`: 699 us/tick.
Clippy is clean.
