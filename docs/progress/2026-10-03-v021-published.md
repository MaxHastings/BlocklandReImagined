## Creator outcome and limits

The packaged `V0.2.1-PLAYTEST.md` gives editable door/team/puzzle/checkpoint/
region/physics recipes, exact-spawner two-ball tests, map-change duplicator and
Admin checks, portal/video comparisons, city firefights and supported NPC rule/
inventory experiments. Blockhead Bot must be enabled in Start Game → Add-Ons.
`RULE-WORKSHOP-DESIGN.md` records materially chosen semantics; audits record
awkward seams and reusable versus experimental pieces. This remains a reversible
playable design spike, not a beta format or universal NPC promise.

The original Windows NaN firefight crash remains **OPEN / not causally fixed**;
intermittent Windows city hangs also need user reproduction. Shark collision and
full original behavior parity remain incomplete. Four independent switch latches
compose and win in the measured fixture; eight exhaust the current planning
budget while combat continues. Exact-ball transport, hookshot route discovery,
opaque Add-On reasoning, autonomous team jobs and cooperative actor stacking are
not delivered. Ordinary actor support/climbing is physically verified, distinct
from autonomous discovery/coordination. Maxwell owns all interactive acceptance.
The full alpha contract remains open.
