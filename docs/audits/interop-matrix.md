# Add-On interop matrix (v0.3.0)

What happens when Add-Ons by different authors meet in one game, checked by
running them together. The Mixed Add-On Playground
(`packages/playground/`) is the content; every row names the test that
proves it. Started 2026-10-12 on `main` after v0.2.10.

Status words:

- **works**: proven by a test with only Add-On data, no engine change.
- **fixed here**: a generic engine defect the Playground found, fixed in
  this change; the test pins the fix.
- **gap**: the engine has no mechanism yet. The workstream that owns it is
  named (see the v0.3.0 plan).

## The Playground Add-Ons

Five Add-Ons, each standing alone and naming no other except through
`depends`. All their art and sound is generated by
`tools/make_playground_assets.py` (deterministic, no third-party assets).

| Add-On | Kind | What it brings |
|---|---|---|
| `playground-runner-look` | model (client) | A box-model character, red cap and blue overalls, 1.9 units tall |
| `playground-runner` | archetype (host) | The Runner body: own size, eyes, speeds and jump, no jets |
| `playground-rifle` | weapons | The Pulse Carbine: own `*.shape.json` model, magazine of 24, reload, projectile, sounds |
| `playground-buggy` | vehicles | A two-seat buggy: own chassis and tires, smashes bricks, driver steers, passenger keeps their weapon |
| `playground-mode` | mode | Plays them together with `blockhead_bot`: Runner body, Carbine loadout, respawn rules |

## Defects found that belong elsewhere

- **A bot that respawns on a player's spawn point freezes.** In the mode,
  with one spawn point, the bot respawns where the player stands and stays
  in `fight` with its leg in `walk`, velocity zero and feet at y 0.05
  forever. Handed to the thread "Bot frozen when spawning on a player". The
  Playground test uses two spawn points until that lands.
- **The buggy pulls left when driven straight.** With the yaw held at the
  wall's centre it drifts to the wall's left corner (x about -5.4). It also
  bounces when someone boards it from above. Tuning or physics, not
  interop; left for a vehicle-feel pass.

## Network test notes

The network test drives in real time, so it asserts what every client must
agree on (the buggy reached the wall, bricks fell, and the driver, watcher
and late joiner see the same buggy pose and the same standing bricks), not
an exact path. A test client that stops reading its socket while another
client drives loses vehicle datagrams; the test keeps the watcher reading,
as a real client does.
