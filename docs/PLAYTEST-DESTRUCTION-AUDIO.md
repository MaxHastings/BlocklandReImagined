# Recorded destruction audio playtest

This candidate adds restrained real-recording plastic sound to the merged
destruction design. It uses the existing volume and Physics Quality settings;
there is no additional audio/destruction switch. All peers need this version.

First shoot a small brick wall and let debris settle. Familiar break sounds
should remain, with a few positional clicks on real impacts. A settled pile
should go quiet rather than rattle indefinitely. Walk through it and listen
for a restrained response when pieces collide again.

Retry the pirate-ship mast: a small snap as its complete assembly begins moving,
then a short scatter when it hits the deck/hull, with selected later clicks.
The streamed member batches should not create a regular snapping metronome.
Try Best and lower Physics Quality; the audio remains capped rather than playing
one sound per brick. Physics Off still gets shared section start/landing cues.

On Afghanistan DM, try an upper floor and a wall corner. Judge position, scale,
volume against the existing rocket/footsteps, and whether repeated blasts remain
pleasant. Farther away, small clicks should disappear before the larger landing
clatter. No extra gameplay explosion is created when a rocket crosses cosmetic
rubble; existing local push reactions remain.

Record whether sounds are too quiet/loud, too sharp/dull, late, repetitive or
seem to come from the wrong spot. Include build version, scene and Physics
Quality. Technical checks do not establish listening or fun acceptance.

Recordings are real LEGO taps/drops/pours by rioforce and C_Sander (CC0).
The snap role is a tap used to suggest a connection releasing. No AI-generated
sounds. Source links, hashes, trims, preview compression and gain processing
are supplied in the bundled package's SOURCES.json/README/LICENSE.
