# v0.3.3 Alpha — repeating water and Add-On aiming controls

This release fixes water coverage on repeating maps, adds a small aiming
presentation control for Add-On authors, and makes the player and creator
guides easier to follow.

## Changes

- Repeated water follows visible terrain beyond the former fixed patch,
  including The Slopes. Finite water and the existing Environment water
  selection remain supported. Authoritative water physics is unchanged.
- Weapon Zoom profiles may declare which image states allow aiming visuals.
  Add-Ons can hide their sights/FOV effect during sprinting or reload and
  restore them in allowed states. Omitting the policy preserves existing
  behavior; retained input ownership prevents unintended jet or zoom fallback.
- The README starts with downloads and launch instructions. Updated player,
  hosting, update/backup, Add-On creator and contributor guides distinguish
  published features, alpha limitations and development work. Useful dated
  research and release evidence remain available.
- Packaged guide links resolve in Windows, macOS and Linux layouts. Offline
  checks cover maintained links/headings, generated guide layouts and notices;
  CI includes the documentation check and its regressions.

## Validation and limits

Release publication requires the combined gate, matching required CI, all three
platform builds, archive manifests/hashes and an extracted Windows startup and
Add-On health check. Headless and offscreen checks do not replace interactive
gameplay acceptance.

Repeated water uses explicit coverage/resource limits. The terrain-mask checks
do not claim complete original underwater or terrain-hole fidelity. Private AA
consumers exercise the aiming policy, but this release does not include a
completed AA2 or Halo game or their original source assets.

The intermittent hosted position pullback and reported mesh-slope feel remain
unreproduced and are not claimed fixed. GSF Paradise's missing source texture
and unsupported rotating map Items remain import limitations. The earlier
Linux wheel-density correction is not full physical reporter revalidation.
AvP remains excluded from public packages; local test copies are retained.
