# v0.3.4 Alpha — bot fixes, join codes and a large set of new Add-On author tools

This release fixes zombie bots using guns, makes bots better at getting around
crowds and doorways, adds join codes and an Invite a friend button, and gives
Add-On authors many new opt-in building blocks. Nothing changes for an Add-On
that does not use them.

## For players

- Zombies and other melee bots no longer pick up or use guns.
- A bot held up by other bodies now waits instead of deciding the way is blocked,
  and a bot shot from out of sight searches for whoever shot it.
- The Game page can show an Add-On's bot count, and the Make a Game picker has
  a plain entry name and an Invite a friend button.
- Join codes work without any online service: the code is the host's address.
  A join step that never answers now fails with its name instead of hanging.
- A new mini-game starts with fresh per-mini-game Add-On state.
- A server stops listening when its endpoint is closed.
- Fixes from the v0.3.3 logs, plus clearer log lines when a player is moved off
  a blocking collider.
- Sky and clouds show from the first frame again after loading.

## For Add-On authors (all opt-in)

- Movement: held-move momentum with floor friction, a walking state, a body that
  follows stairs and slopes, a wall-step over a lip, an unstick that moves a body
  closed in by geometry, and a cylinder player body.
- Weapons and hits: hitscan trace strength (ricochet, penetration, stop by
  surface), exit landings, per-bone hit cylinders with damage multipliers and a
  body region, and script raycasts that report map movers.
- Look and lighting: projected pictures and decals on the map, map backdrops,
  regions with their own fog and ambient light, lit static meshes and moving
  bodies, layer-blended and animated surfaces, scatter layers, and positional
  looping map sounds with walls and rooms cutting a sound's range.
- Panels and HUD: image panels, backgrounds and textured buttons, a heading-strip
  marker, and rule-driven controls layers with binds that win over a profile.
- Physics and rules: joint cone and hinge, ghost bodies, freeze, push at a point,
  an optional volume on rule sounds, and named log and warn lines for rules.
- Bots: objectives for work-a-place and guard tasks, ladders and map doors, and
  readouts that say why a bot noticed nothing or stopped.

## Validation and limits

Release publication requires the combined gate, matching required CI, all three
platform builds, archive manifests/hashes and an extracted Windows startup and
Add-On health check. Headless and offscreen checks do not replace interactive
gameplay acceptance; the maintainer played normal Blockland on this engine.

Bot pathing through one-brick doorways and narrow corridors changed in this
release and was not separately compared against v0.3.3 beyond that playtest.
