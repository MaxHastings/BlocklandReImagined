# v0.4.3 Alpha

- Voice and eligible chat can continue during accepted loading. Loading also
  supports chat scrolling, the player list and options. A minigame can choose
  Global voice so its living and dead members can talk together.
- A creator can leave a minigame and rejoin while its battle continues. Slayer's
  Reset When Empty option remains available for games that want that behavior.
- Improved bot pursuit, objective routing, stair and doorway navigation,
  remembered searches and supported jumping. Bot Hole controls include bot
  power, naming, search and wander settings, and an authored idle-player seeking
  option. Bot spawns follow their brick's orientation.
- Destroyed-brick lights, emitters and brick music follow their visible carrier;
  destroyed sources suspend new item and actor spawns until restored.
- Ordinary transparent surfaces and decals respond to lighting, and brick
  materials have subtle specular response under supported lights.
- Adjusted hammer, wrench and sword swing sounds and ambience levels. The
  outdoor ambience base uses the selected car-park recording. Music played by
  rules supports per-play gain.
- Improved Bot_Town's declared brick-geometry dependency import and fixed the
  reproduced loading-channel exit, along with window-resolution handling and
  death-body collision corrections.

For modders, the existing voice capability exposes bounded per-listener routing,
gain and filtering controls; Add-Ons provide their own radio effects. Rule music
gain and optional brick normal maps build on existing effects and material seams.
See [engine seams](../architecture/seams.md) for API ownership and evidence.

Alpha remains incomplete. The gradual movement delay that can clear on respawn
or F8/F7 has no proven cause; the reported unswung hammer flicker and a friend's
server restart are also not established as fixed. Bots can still choose a
difficult route or stall, especially around balconies and crowds; the tested
cube can be reached without jets, but arbitrary-height stacking is not promised.
BadDream's animated-light behavior and full v21 lighting parity remain open.
Some bundled legacy Add-Ons report unsupported script or brick conversions in
their health reports. Materials without authored normal maps use defaults, and
not every game texture has unique bump detail. The updated audio balance has
not had a new combined listening test.
