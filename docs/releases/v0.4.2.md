# v0.4.2 Alpha

- Improved bot navigation, vehicle interaction and recovery. Temporary brick
  restoration waits when its exact space is occupied by a player or vehicle.
- Improved contact and recovery for bounded brick debris, including support,
  nearby actors and restoration under capacity pressure.
- Added the approved indoor/outdoor ambience bases across non-Storm maps,
  including transitions into player-built rooms. Slate Storm keeps its existing
  mix. Kitchen's fridge and Creek's authored waterfall and shore sounds remain
  localized; unrelated bird, frog and cricket layers are inactive.
- Added three approved variations for hammer and wrench swings through their
  existing animation states. Original contact sounds remain intact.
- Reduced the harshness of water's sun glare using its original texture. The
  original texture, color, movement, swimming, buoyancy and hazards are preserved.
- Fixed automatic Add-On imports rejecting their own staging folder. Added
  clearer compatibility notes for missing dependencies and unsupported behavior.
- Fixed legacy map resource paths and reuse of exactly matching installed
  vanilla sky assets, with integrity checks and authored map settings preserved.

Maxwell approved the combined playtest and the final ambience mix. That human
acceptance is separate from the platform, content and release checks recorded in
the publication receipt.

Modders can author intrinsic SoundDef spatial distances and interior/exterior
intent. The listener applies bounded physical shelter sampling and smoothed
gain/filter changes while preserving authored region acoustics. Legacy map
conversion can reuse an exactly matching installed sky with integrity checks.
See the [source-checked seams](../architecture/seams.md) for API availability.

Alpha remains incomplete. Custom legacy scripts do not automatically become
native gameplay: compatibility notes describe remaining gaps. The supplied
zombie and Roll gameplay ports are deferred. Bot behavior in tight spaces and
large destruction scenarios still have limits. This release does not change
stock Gun/Akimbo brick policy or add vehicle engine recordings.
