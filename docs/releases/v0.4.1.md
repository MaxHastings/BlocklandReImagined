# Blockland ReImagined v0.4.1 Alpha

Voice conversation rules and speaking feedback, independent tank gunner aim,
cleaner player lists, and a correction for physical debris after repeated hits.

## Voice conversations

- The Voice Chat Add-On owns conversation rules: players outside minigames
  can talk globally to other outside players; minigame conversations use
  proximity within that minigame. Separate minigames stay isolated. Host
  settings can change voice mode and distance.
- Dead minigame players speak to other dead members by default. Living
  members can still be heard by dead members. Spectating does not move the
  speaker's origin to another player's location.
- A compact speaking list above chat identifies eligible speakers, shows
  channel labels and an overflow count, and fades after speech stops. Add-On
  HUD customization can hide or replace it.
- Received voice is 50% louder at the same volume setting. Microphone gain
  and mute controls retain their behavior; the crowd mix limiter remains.
- Add-On authors can declare bounded voice routing with the voice
  capability. Optional radio samples demonstrate channels and a held radio
  binding using the ordinary microphone controls.
- Route generations reject obsolete audio after conversation changes and
  clear buffered speech when a route is revoked.

## Vehicles, lists and debris

- Turning a tank's hull no longer turns the gunner's chosen world heading.
  Boarding still begins with the view aligned to the barrel.
- Ordinary player lists, their counts and administration targets show human
  players. Bots remain available in minigame team assignment tools.
- A collapse waiting for physics capacity can regain physical breakup after
  earlier debris expires. Prepared landing geometry is retained while it
  waits, instead of permanently becoming a visual-only assembly. Existing
  body and geometry limits remain in effect.
- Joining resolves the declared server companion interfaces used by client
  controls, including New Duplicator bindings. Server scripts stay on the host.
- NPC reactions initialize missing per-bot voice state during ordinary
  spawning, damage, death and respawn, avoiding a script error on live bots.

## Alpha limits and playtest

Updated clients and servers are required for the voice protocol change. Echo
cancellation and noise suppression remain unavailable; use headphones with
voice activation. Optional radio samples are examples, not enabled defaults.
Rubble remains client presentation and does not crush players or block
authoritative projectiles. This patch does not claim to solve every
destruction hitch or establish acceptance of the older pirate-ship preview.

For a focused playtest: turn a tank while another player holds a steady
gunner aim; check ordinary player/admin lists and bot team assignment; talk
outside and inside separate minigames, including death and mute transitions;
then repeat hits on a supported mast/sail and walk into its landed debris.
Interactive acceptance remains Maxwell's, and the full alpha roadmap is open.
