# v0.3.2 definition of done

Maxwell's latest October 16 direction supersedes the showcase release hold:
**finish the reported bug fixes, include completed engine seams that pass the
integrated gate, then publish v0.3.2.** Do not wait for Halo/AA fidelity, a new
mechanism or a percentage estimate. Freeze the selected candidate at immutable
reviewed heads; incomplete branches remain separate and continue with owners.

Chief/AR/Warthog and AA M16 acceptance below remain active fidelity goals, but
H1-H4, N1 and A1 no longer block this bug-fix release. Shotgun stays independent;
GMod remains parked. Existing gameplay must not regress in the selected changes.

v0.3.1 is already published at `5956e3a383a1c241bd5192a3aa8cc4f358f2e503`.
Do not replace its tag or artifacts. Maxwell now authorizes automatic v0.3.2
publication after bug-fix, integrated, required CI/platform and package checks;
another approval or final interactive playtest is not a publication prerequisite.
Failed checks and known deterministic release blockers still hold publication.
This does not declare the showcase or complete vanilla alpha roadmap finished.

## What counts as evidence

For every required row, retain the source reference, exact engine and private
package commits, ordinary input sequence, authoritative observations, native
consumer/render evidence where relevant, failure controls and result receipt.
Keep committed capabilities distinct from local edits and scoped checks distinct
from integrated acceptance. Imported geometry, an isolated render, a typed API
or a passing helper test is an intermediate checkpoint.

At each meaningful capability boundary, exercise one affected consumer through
ordinary authoritative gameplay before expanding the dependent mechanism.
Keep the successful scenario as a regression. Small helpers use normal test
tiers; independent source/private work may continue during validation.
Missing behavior returns to its engine owner as an executable counterexample;
do not hide it with a package timer, fake completion, replacement asset or
game-specific engine branch. New scope needs a demonstrated consumer need,
source evidence, exact ownership/path reservation and root review.

Rows remain OPEN until their own evidence closes them. Showcase rows are
continuing work, while reliability triage/fixes and R1-R3 govern this release.
Unreproduced reports retain their evidence limits; do not claim them fixed or
guess physics/input changes merely to mark a box complete.
