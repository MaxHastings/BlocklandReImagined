# v0.3.7 Alpha — ragdolls, body lighting, footsteps, soft sun shadows and a much bigger HUD toolkit

## For players

- **Soft Sun Shadows (PCSS).** A new Graphics option gives sun shadows soft,
  distance-aware edges. It is part of the quality presets, and the Graphics
  screen is now tidied into effect columns in a sensible order. Thanks to
  Sirdelorus for the original contribution.
- **Ragdoll bodies.** Characters go limp when they die, and the hit that killed
  them carries into the fall. Bodies can lie for a set time and fade out, or
  stay until their player spawns again.
- **Hit zones for every posture.** Standing, crouched and prone characters each
  have their own hit bones, so shots land where the body actually is.
- **Better-lit characters.** Bodies are lit by the lamps and suns that can
  actually reach them, in the style of classic Direct3D lighting, and they keep
  their light correctly at a lamp's edge. Doors and other baked moving parts
  keep their vertex colours (no more white doors) and still take live lights.
- **Footsteps and movement sound.** Steps take their sound and loudness from the
  surface and from the character's animation, so walking, crouching and crawling
  each sound like themselves. Jumps sound at take-off, and feet leave footprints
  and dust where they land. Bots hear footsteps and landings.
- **Ladders.** Proper climb animations, rung sounds taken from the map, and
  other players see you climbing.
- **Map display settings.** A Brightness, Contrast and Gamma display ramp, in
  the Unreal Engine 2 manner, is a client setting; maps can supply their own
  defaults under it.
- **Faster big maps.** Maps that describe their zones draw only the zones the
  eye can see into.
- **Reflections indoors** now see the room around them rather than the open sky.
- **Glass and other blended surfaces** now receive decals.
- **Server and menus.** Server menus stay reachable through loading and held
  spawns; admins can leave the server game; casings come to rest by default;
  drops can linger; bots are quieter; the console can report your location and
  clipboard editing works; the admin camera keys work in custom HUDs.
- **Bigger encounters.** Finite bot encounters support up to 64 bots with
  consistent participant limits across admission and replication.
- **Fixes.** Room lighting transitions, ladder bounds and stopped-door collision
  were corrected, and long frames no longer make gun casings vanish.

## For builders and Add-On authors

- **HUD toolkit.** HUD layouts can declare their own frame size. A radar/map
  element shows a panned, turned, zoomable map with marks. Panels gain button
  actions, text controls, placed text and list items, dark list fields with
  their own text colour, two-condition show chains, value-picked colours and
  diagonal edge-arrow sectors.
- **Weapons.** Stances, tool put-down, 3D sights, an eject cue, mover and sound
  options, a tracer every Nth round, instant zoom snap, image states that follow
  whether the holder is aiming, and a fire button that stays held when a rule
  takes an empty-hand press. A missing muzzle or eject node is reported once.
- **Shots through bodies.** A traced shot can pass through a body and lose
  strength by its thickness, with surface `depth` costing strength too.
- **Explosions** can kill outright within a kill radius.
- **Bodies.** Archetypes can describe ragdoll bones, shapes, mass, damping and
  joints, set how long a dead body lies, and dress each spawn with other
  textures and a draw-only scale. Footfall marks in a clip name the sounds and
  loudness of each step, and a posture can have its own footfalls.
- **Bot awareness.** Rules are told what bots perceive: gunfire, fire past
  them, grenades, allies dying, lost enemies and empty guns, plus when a bot
  takes a new target (`on_bot_spotted`).
- **Ragdoll joints.** Ball joints can have a soft cone limit (`cone_stiffness`,
  `cone_damping`), as skeletal joints behave in Karma. A crouched body's damage
  multipliers (`crouch_damage`) are archetype data.
- **Sound and screen rules.** Rules can query a sound profile's declared length
  (`sound_seconds`), turn the world's sounds down for a while (`world_volume`)
  and draw a curve-driven full-screen overlay (`screen_overlay`).
- **Radar marks.** A HUD radar mark can be drawn larger than the panel's mark
  size (`scale`).
- **Rules.** A behaviour can load several script files as one script. Movers,
  placed items, tool swapping and state locks are available to rules.
- **Models.** A glTF node with `bri_projector` bakes a decal.
- **Maps.** The importer can split a world mesh by zone and list the zone graph.
- **Limits.** Content size limits are sized for real Add-Ons and name the limit
  when one trips.
- **Developer tooling.** Capture scripts can press keys and mouse buttons, aim
  at a player, wait for a death, and report the held gun's ammo and image
  states. Render stats count world draws by material kind. Pull requests now run
  a quick test tier on Linux, with the full tiers on Windows after merge.

## Known limits

Everything here is alpha. Ragdoll tuning, body lighting and footstep mixes were
checked against source and automated captures, not by a long interactive
playtest, and Maxwell performs interactive playtesting. Original Blockland and
other third-party game assets are not part of this repository or its release.
The RX550 black-world report remains unconfirmed, and the forced FXC software
renderer limitation noted in v0.3.5 still stands.
