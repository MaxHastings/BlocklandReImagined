# v0.4.6 Alpha

- Stock and imported models compile to GLB with their original detail
  levels, object visibility, animation and collision assignments intact.
  Add-On authors can continue editing and importing ordinary glTF or GLB
  source. The game validates the compiled models when it reads them.
- Dynamic lighting softens map sun shadows along with sun shadows from
  bricks, players and vehicles. Dynamic and Unified blend the fitted
  shadows into a coarser map at distance. Classic lighting retains its
  existing caster fade.
- Voice Activity Detection continues in an active session while the game is
  unfocused or minimized. Push-to-talk releases on focus loss. Host mutes
  and microphone settings still govern transmission.
- The Add-Ons list has distinct category headers and category-wide
  checkboxes. A mixed checkbox enables eligible members. Individual
  checkboxes, protected Add-Ons and dependency confirmations are preserved.

The model pipeline adds a shared compiled GLB reader for the importer,
renderer and host. See the [glTF authoring guide](../modding/gltf-models.md)
and [engine seams](../architecture/seams.md).

The 124 checked v20 model detail tables match the same source assets,
including all ten levels on the Kitchen palms and oak. Distant shadow
quality depends on the coarse map's coverage and resolution. Device
behavior and visual acceptance remain player checks.
