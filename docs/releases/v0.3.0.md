Blockland ReImagined v0.3.0 Alpha: the engine learns to run very different Add-Ons side by side. Add-On bodies with their own movement, animations, camera, HUD and sounds, Blender models, and vehicles and bots that work with all of them.

## New for Add-On makers
- **Add-On movement controllers.** A body can bring its own movement code, run as WebAssembly on top of the v20 motor. It is predicted on the client and authoritative on the host, so a body can jump, dive or wall-kick its own way and still play smoothly online.
- **Bodies hold, aim, sit and animate by their own sockets and clips.** Weapons sit in a body's own hands, seats use its own sitting pose with hands on the handles, and missing clips fall back sensibly.
- **Switching bodies mid-game.** A brick event or a rules command can turn a player into any Add-On body. The body survives respawns and joins.
- **Blender (glTF/GLB) models** import as bodies, weapons and vehicles, and reach joining players like any other Add-On file. See `docs/modding/gltf-models.md`.
- **HUD regions and cameras.** An Add-On can own parts of the HUD (an ammo counter, for example) and its body's camera.
- **Add-On sounds by file.** Bodies and weapons can play their own sound files for jumping, pain and death.
- **Bots that know each body.** Bot profiles, intents and abilities, body contact and per-body reach. Bots run and jump the way their body does.
- **Add-Ons that build on each other.** A rule can export functions and send events other Add-Ons listen to, and keep data on the host across restarts, filed per player. Roleplay banks, inventories and Wiremod-style logic can be written now. `sample-rp-bank`, `sample-rp-inventory` and `sample-wire-lite` show how.
- **Windows, hooks and groups for rules.** Rules can open panels with text, lists, fields and buttons, with rows dragged between lists. They can hear objects being created and removed and two vehicles crashing, and put bodies in pass groups so teammates walk through each other. Client code can place the camera and take over game controls.
- **Modder tools.** `/tune` changes a body's numbers live for every player of it and saves them back to its file. The game explains why an Add-On isn't in a game, and tells you when a file changed while you host. `bri-addon-check --package` packs an Add-On as one ZIP.
- **Vehicle engine sounds**, and sound files checked with the game's own decoder.
- **Mixed Add-On Playground.** Its own Add-Ons (the Stomper, bot bodies, spawns from bricks, a buggy with a gunner seat) show and test all of the above together.

## Gameplay and fixes
- **Vehicles feel better.** Tuned handling, steering by look, and several riders in one vehicle without rubber-banding. Parked vehicles sleep once they sit still, which cuts the host's work on busy maps.
- **Portals cut collision in two.** A vehicle or player halfway through a portal collides on both sides: with what's in front of it, and with what's past the partner portal.
- **Smarter bots.** In Capture the Flag a carrier runs home, a team recovers its own dropped flag, and bots take a vehicle when driving beats walking. Bots face the way they walk, spread over different routes, and use their jets to break a fall. 32 bots on a dense build cost about 9% less host time.
- **One Mini-Game window** with Game, Teams, Rules, Players and More Settings tabs. Team loadouts, team lives and mode resets work.
- **Environment window:** Look, Sky and day cycle no longer fight each other.
- **Fractional UI scales** draw sharply.
- **Fixes from the release gate:** a body with its own movement no longer sinks through floors or creeps upward while standing. Riders are seated only by landing on a vehicle, as in v20. `/botreload` in a game mode keeps only the bots the mode can run.
- **Impact decals** on bricks.
- **No mid-match freezes** from body mesh uploads and mouse handling.
- **Joining players can chat** while the map loads.
- **Stereo world sounds play in 3D** (the Mini-Nuke and Add-On weapons).
- Everything from v0.2.12.

## Players on older versions
- v0.3.0 changes the network protocol, so everyone has to update: v0.2.x players cannot join a v0.3.0 game, and v0.3.0 players cannot join theirs.

## Known issues
- A body can't supply its own first-person arms yet. In first person you see the held weapon alone, as in v20.
- A vehicle driven off the map falls forever, as in v20.
- Vehicles can't join a pass group yet.
- A car driven into a portal can pass through a player standing halfway into the other opening, instead of hitting them.
- See [KNOWN-ISSUES.md](https://github.com/MaxHastings/BlocklandReImagined/blob/v0.3.0/docs/KNOWN-ISSUES.md).

## Download

| Windows x86-64 | `BlocklandReImagined-windows.zip` | `Launch.cmd` |
| macOS Apple silicon | `BlocklandReImagined-macos.zip` | `BlocklandReImagined.app` (ad-hoc signed) |

Extract into a fresh folder. Settings and saves stay in your normal user profile. Host multiplayer by IP; internet hosting needs UDP port 28000 reachable. `SHA256SUMS` has the archive hashes.
