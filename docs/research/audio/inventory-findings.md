## Method and uncertainty

- Scripts were tokenised with a read-only scanner (`crates/audio-import/src/tscript.rs`).
  Only literal fields, literal globals and simple `$var @ "text"`
  concatenations are resolved. The one dynamic construct that matters, the
  `eval`-built music datablocks, is modelled explicitly from its source.
- Engine behaviour not expressed in script (attenuation, gain curve, voice
  limits) comes from TGE-family source, not from the Blockland binary. It is
  flagged as such in [runtime-model.md](runtime-model.md).
- The add-on load order used for duplicates is an assumption. Every current
  duplicate resolves to identical bytes either way.
