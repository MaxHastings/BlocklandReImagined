# v0.2.3 Alpha release notes

Published as [v0.2.3 Alpha](https://github.com/MaxHastings/BlocklandReImagined/releases/tag/v0.2.3).
[Publication evidence](../progress/2026-10-04-v023-published-and-cleanup.md)
records the final gates and archive checks. The [player checks](V0.2.3-PLAYTEST.md)
cover the main changes; [known issues](../KNOWN-ISSUES.md) retain the wider limits.

- **MiniGame drafts and favorites:** missing bodies/tools retain their identity
  as Unavailable until you explicitly recover the choice. Combined normal/Add-On
  settings wait for their actual acknowledgments, report partial failures, and
  protect drafts while saving. Unsupported favorite settings are disclosed and
  the source favorite is protected from accidental overwrite. Saved builds
  restore declared per-MiniGame Add-On state in the loaded game's scope.
- **Administration and joining:** administration recovers from a demonstrated
  stale-refresh ordering race. Guests prepare fetched Add-Ons before admission,
  avoiding a temporary connect/leave/rejoin during content preparation.
- **Preferences:** explicit Add-On choices now persist by identity in the normal
  user profile across fresh release folders. Default restores the current build's
  defaults. Choices lost with an older discarded folder cannot be reconstructed.
- **Vehicles and portals:** Wrench Send recolors the existing spawned vehicle
  without respawning it. Gravity Gun access to linked spawn objects now uses
  the existing builder/MiniGame ownership policy; this does not bypass game rules.
  Mounted actor travel and driven-view crossing use the
  existing portal mechanics; first-person portal views suppress the local body
  copy at its own eye. Authoritative travel frames settle accepted/rejected crossings and control changes, including dismounts and stationary vehicle updates.
- **Bots and creator feedback:** repeated physical-object goals require observed
  exit/reentry; actual native holds and charged attacks retain their commitment
  through supported temporary interruptions. Explain keeps NPC/event feedback
  scoped to the inspected brick. Supported objectives still execute ordinary
  controls and require observed results.
- **Building and appearance:** map surfaces no longer supply blocked stud support
  to otherwise invalid placements/copies; long copy work rechecks live authority
  and support. Sparse meshes use actual opaque face coverage for hiding brick
  faces. Avatar options animate the native preview with corrected lighting.
  Dynamic lighting respects authored unlit map materials and their opaque
  shadow participation, while Classic/Unified keep their existing appearance.
- **Shark and imported content:** standalone death types and icons survive import.
  Shark capture completion retains its identity through ordinary typed damage.
  Imported brick shapes borrowed from another Add-On are included under their
  own stable bindings; Shark/Zombie holes load without the lender’s native catalog.

The Windows firefight NaN crash, reported death/disconnect, destruction/respawn
hitches and tank retreat/cover behavior have not been causally fixed. Death
correction diagnostics are more precise; passing normal lifecycle tests are not
a reproduction of the reported disconnect. No Windows speedup is claimed.
Arbitrary jet carrying, high-hoop throws and undeclared Add-On semantics remain
unsupported. Shark/Zombie behavior and recovered interior lighting remain partial.
Interactive acceptance belongs to Maxwell, and the full alpha contract is open.
