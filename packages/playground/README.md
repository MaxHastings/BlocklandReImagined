# Mixed Add-On Playground

Twelve Add-Ons written as if by different people, to prove that Add-Ons which
never heard of each other play together. Ships in `default-addons.json`
turned off. What each combination does, and what is still missing, is in
[docs/audits/interop-matrix.md](../../docs/audits/interop-matrix.md).

| Folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| `playground-runner-look` | The Runner's box-model body (client) |
| `playground-runner` | The Runner: its size, eyes, speeds and jump |
| `playground-rifle` | The Pulse Carbine weapon |
| `playground-buggy` | A dune buggy with driver, passenger and gunner seats that smashes bricks |
| `playground-runner-hud` | The Runner's own textured HUD (client) |
| `playground-stomper-look` | The Stomper's box-model body (client) |
| `playground-stomper` | A simple enemy that walks up and stomps you; its body is also a player type |
| `playground-stomper-rules` | What touching a Stomper means (host): flattened when landed on or run over |
| `playground-fizzer-look` | The Fizzer's box-model body (client) |
| `playground-fizzer` | A creature that walks up, hisses and pops, breaking bricks; its body, kind, blast and sounds |
| `playground-fizzer-rules` | What the Fizzer's pop does (host, `on_bot_ability`) |
| `playground-mode` | The mode that plays them together, with Blockhead bots, Stompers, Fizzers and `sample-trooper` |

The others name no other Add-On (the Runner, the Stomper and the Fizzer depend only on their looks, their rules on them,
and the Runner's HUD only on the Runner).
Only the mode names them all. Models, textures and sounds are generated by
`python tools/make_playground_assets.py`; rerun it after changing its
numbers. Nothing here comes from another game.

Tests: `cargo test -p bri-sim --test playground` (one server, every
combination) and `cargo test -p bri-net --test playground` (two players and
a late joiner over the network).

## Playtest (about five minutes)

1. In Add-Ons, turn on the eleven Playground Add-Ons and Blockhead Bot. Host a
   server with the **Mixed Add-On Playground** game mode, then place a
   vehicle spawn with the Dune Buggy and another with a Blockhead Bot.
2. You spawn as the Runner (red cap, blue overalls). Check that it walks,
   jumps and crouches at its own speeds and has no jets.
3. Equip the Pulse Carbine from slot 1. Fire, empty the magazine and reload,
   listening for the shot, empty and reload sounds.
4. Build a wall of plates, jump onto the buggy from above to take the
   driver's seat, and drive through the wall. Bricks should break, and come
   back after 30 seconds.
5. With two more players: one boards as passenger and fires the Carbine,
   the other stands at the rear gun and clicks to fire it, while you drive.
6. Place a vehicle spawn with a Stomper. It should wander, walk up to you
   with empty hands and stomp you about once a second; Stompers never
   fight each other.
7. Place a vehicle spawn with a Fizzer beside a wall of plates. It should
   wander, walk up to you, flash and hiss for a moment and pop, hurting
   you and knocking bricks out of the wall. Run away while it hisses and it
   stops and follows you instead.
   Jump onto a Stomper's cap, or run one over with the buggy: it is
   flattened.
8. Let a bot shoot you. You should respawn as the Runner holding the
   Carbine.
7. As the Runner, the top of the screen shows a gold power meter that
   loses wedges as you are hurt, a cap with a health counter, and a gold
   reticle instead of the crosshair. Draw the Carbine: a big gold-edged
   round count, a magazine bar and the reserve sit bottom right instead of
   the text ammo display, counting down as you fire and showing RELOAD
   while you reload. Type `/trooper`: the HUD goes back to
   the stock one, the view narrows, it bobs as you walk, the gun trails
   your turns, and the third-person camera sits over the right shoulder.
   Type `/blockhead`: the camera is the stock one again.

Known: the Carbine floats beside a box body instead of in its hand, shots
start at the eye, and the buggy drifts left when driven straight. These are
listed in the matrix with the workstream that fixes them.
